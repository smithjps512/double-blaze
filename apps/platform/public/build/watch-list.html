A word about watching versus doing

It is genuinely possible to watch six hours of tutorials and be no better at this than when you started. It feels like learning because it is somebody competent doing the thing, and watching somebody competent is pleasant.

The rule that works: watch a video once, then go and do the thing badly. Being stuck for ten minutes on your own app teaches you more than another hour of somebody else's. Then, when you are stuck on something specific, come back and watch the ten seconds that covers it.

That is also why every page here is written for your project rather than a generic one. A tutorial shows somebody else's app. Your architecture page has your button names on it.
