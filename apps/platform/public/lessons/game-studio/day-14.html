Day 14: Bad day scenarios

At the end of today, every level has a bad day scenario, and at least one of them is the lose condition.

Again (5 minutes)

  1. Read one happy path out loud, Given, When, Then, without looking.

Watch (5 to 10 minutes)

The teacher does this on the projector, start to finish. Nobody types until it is done.

  1. Say: QA Leads are paid to be weird. Ask the room: what weird things do players do in games? (Walk backwards, jump at walls, stand still, press every button.) Write them on the board.
  2. Open Demo Studio's level 1. Press + Bad day. Type: Given Bolt is at the edge of a gap, When the player jumps too late, Then Bolt falls, loses a heart, and restarts at the last checkpoint.
  3. Add a weird one for level 3: Given the timer is running, When the player stands still, Then the magnets pull Bolt in and the timer keeps going.
  4. Point at the Core loop tab's lose condition. Say: one of your bad days should be how the player loses, written exactly.

Say out loud: every bug a QA tester finds is a bad day nobody wrote down. Write them now, before the build.

Do (25 to 30 minutes)

  1. (again) Open your studio. Check every level has a happy path.
  2. (today) Add a bad day to every level. The QA Lead leads today.
  3. (today) One bad day must be your lose condition: what exactly happens when the player loses?
  4. (today) Add one weird one: what if the player does something you did not plan for?
  5. (today) Check the Definition of Done on each level. Write down which ones are not green yet.

Stuck? Take the happy path and change one word in the When: too early, too late, the wrong button, nothing at all.

Done when

Every level has a bad day, one of them is the lose condition, and the studio knows which levels are not done yet.
