Day 5: Hand it over

At the end of today, every team's file has been shared from their design brief, read by the review against their own documents, and one finding has been fixed and confirmed gone.

Again (5 minutes)

From memory, faster:

  1. Open Handing over your design and go straight to the checklist at the bottom. Run it against your file. Fix what is quick.

Watch (5 to 10 minutes)

  1. Share Bus Buddy's file in Figma with your own school email as a viewer. Say: not "anyone with the link". Copy link.
  2. Open Bus Buddy's design brief, scroll to Share your design, paste the link, type one line about what is done, press Share it. Read the message. Say: nothing changed. It went to me.
  3. Open Claude Code and run /design-review sample-bus-buddy. Narrate: it reads the link, lists the file's pages, reads each one, compares names with names, writes the page. Say: no model is judging the design. It is a list of names on one side against a list of names on the other.
  4. Read the findings it prints. Take the first one, open the Figma file beside it, and point at the thing it names. Say: that is a finding. Two documents disagreeing, and you can go and look.
  5. Merge its pull request. Say: three minutes. While it deploys, fix that first finding in Figma.
  6. When it lands, open the brief, point at Design review in the chain, open it. Share the file again, run the review again, show the finding gone and the list shorter.

Say out loud: five days ago this was one sentence with a "so that". Now it is a screen a builder can build, checked against that sentence. That is the whole loop, and from here you run it yourselves, one story at a time.

Do (25 to 30 minutes)

Your teacher runs the review for each team during this time. Share early.

  1. (again) The handover checklist, run, quick fixes made.
  2. (today) Share the file in Figma with your teacher's school email as a viewer. Copy the link.
  3. (today) On your design brief, Share your design: the link and one honest line. Press Share it. Read the message.
  4. (today) While you wait, predict the findings. Which name does not match? Which state did you not draw? Write three guesses.
  5. (today) When your review is in your chain, open it beside your Figma file. For every finding, go and look. True or not true. Compare with your guesses.
  6. (today) Fix the finding at the top. Share again, with a note saying what you fixed. When the next review comes, confirm it is gone.
  7. (today) Set your card on the project board to what is now true.

Stuck? The helper on your design brief page has your review open now. Ask it what a finding means before you decide it is wrong.

Done when

Design review in your chain, one finding fixed and gone on the next review, and your board row set to what is true.

After this

The next story starts at Day 1 and takes less than a day. Rewrite or write, send, your teacher approves, the card arrives, design the screen, name the layers, share, fix what the review finds. The review is free and it never gets tired. When it says nothing disagrees, that file is the one that gets built.
