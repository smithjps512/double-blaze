Day 3: The screen, started with AI

At the end of today, every team has one frame in their Figma file, named exactly as their design brief names the screen, with every Done when line on their card visible on it and nothing on it that is not.

Again (5 minutes)

From memory, faster:

  1. From your board, open your build cards page and find your card.
  2. From the chain, open the design brief. Find the screen your card needs and read its component names. Those names are Day 4.

Watch (5 to 10 minutes)

  1. Open Bus Buddy's design brief, which exists as of last night. Point at the screen for yesterday's story and its list of components. Say: this is what the builder is going to build. The design has to have these names on it or the builder cannot find anything.
  2. Open Start with AI and scroll to the prompt. Open Bus Buddy's card beside it. Fill the prompt in from the card, out loud: the screen name, the as a, the I want, the so that, one line per Done when. Read the last paragraph of the prompt and say: that paragraph is the one people skip, and it is the one that stops the gradients.
  3. Run it in whichever AI the school has: Figma Make, First Draft inside the editor, or if neither, draw the frame yourself following Part C. One screen. Do not ask for the app.
  4. Read it back. Go down the card's Done when list with a finger and find each line on the screen. Then point at one thing on the screen that is not on the card. Delete it. Point at a number the AI made up. Say: nobody in our story said 4.8 stars. Replace it with a real word from the story.
  5. Get it on the canvas in Bus Buddy's file, on a page named Card 1 · and the card's title. Name the frame exactly as the brief names the screen. Say: the page name is the card, the frame name is the screen. Those two names are how the review finds it on Day 5.

Say out loud: the AI did the boring part. The design work is everything you just watched me delete.

Do (25 to 30 minutes)

One screen per team. One person prompts, one reads the card, one holds the brief.

  1. (again) Card and brief open side by side.
  2. (today) Write the prompt from your card, in the shape on Start with AI. Every Done when line becomes one line of "on this screen you can see". Keep the last paragraph.
  3. (today) Run it. One screen. If you have no AI, draw it from Part C: one frame, the boxes stacked, and move on.
  4. (today) Read back. For every Done when line, point at it on the screen. Missing? Add it, plainly. On the screen but not on the card? Delete it. Made-up number or name? Replace it with a real one from your story or remove it.
  5. (today) Put it in your team's Figma file: a page named Card N · and your card's title, exactly. The frame named exactly as the brief names the screen. If your brief is not approved yet, name the frame after the screen your card describes, and check it against the brief tomorrow.
  6. (today) Look at it at phone width. If it does not fit, you asked for too much. Cut, do not shrink.

Stuck? Press the I am designing in Figma tab on your design brief page and ask. It has your brief open. It cannot see your Figma file. Tomorrow's questions about names, it can answer today.

Done when

One frame, named as the brief names the screen, on a page named as the card is named, with every Done when line on it and nothing else.

Tomorrow adds
