Day 1: One story, sent

At the end of today, every team has one story in the teacher's inbox, written in the story studio, with criteria that can be checked by looking and one complete Given, When, Then.

Again (5 minutes)

There is no yesterday. Instead, open your team's test plan page from the project board and read it. Find the story with the thinnest test plan under it. That story is the one you take through the week, unless you have a feature with no story at all, in which case that is.

Watch (5 to 10 minutes)

The teacher does this on the projector, start to finish. Nobody types until it is done.

  1. Open Bus Buddy's project board and point at Write a story in the links across the top. Click it. Say: it opened with Bus Buddy already picked, because you came from Bus Buddy's page.
  2. Fill in the boxes, out loud, for a story Bus Buddy is missing. Name it in two words. As a: a kind of person, not a name. I want. So that. Say the "so that" out loud twice: it has to say what goes wrong without this.
  3. Watch the right side. Point at the checks as they change from Needed to done. Point at the test plan filling itself in as you type a criterion. Then type a criterion that cannot be seen, like "it should be fast", and show the test plan not moving. Say: that line is not a criterion, because nobody could check it by looking. Delete it and write one they could.
  4. Write one Given, When, Then. Show the test plan gain a scenario.
  5. Ask Spark one thing in the box at the bottom: "is my so that any good". Read the answer. Say: it asked me a question back. It will not write the story, and that is on purpose. A story it wrote would not be ours.
  6. Send it. Read the message on the screen. Say: nothing has changed anywhere. It went to me.
  7. Open your email on the projector. Show the message with the story in it and the two links. Do not click them. Say: that is tomorrow.

Say out loud: a story is not finished when it is written. It is finished when somebody else can tell whether it came true.

Do (25 to 30 minutes)

One story per team. Three jobs: one types, one reads the checks and the test plan out loud, one keeps the story honest against the plan. Swap after ten minutes if you like.

  1. (today) Decide which story. A thin one you already have, or a feature with none. One.
  2. (today) If it is a rewrite: open your build cards page, scroll to the box headed Your story is wrong?, pick the story, and rewrite it there. If it is new: open Write a story from your board.
  3. (today) Fill in every box. Read Writing a story sections 1 to 3 if a box is hard. The "so that" is the hard one.
  4. (today) Write at least two criteria you could check by looking, and one complete Given, When, Then. Watch the test plan as you go. If it goes thin, your story went thin first.
  5. (today) Ask Spark one question. Not "write it for me". Something like "why does my criterion not show in the test plan".
  6. (today) Go down the list under "Before you send one" on the writing page. Somebody who did not type it reads the whole story out loud.
  7. (today) Send it. Read the message. Write down the time you sent it.

Stuck? The Ask box on the studio page has your team's plan open. Ask it what kind of person your "as a" should be.

Done when

One story sent, and your teacher can see it in the inbox with your team's name on it.
