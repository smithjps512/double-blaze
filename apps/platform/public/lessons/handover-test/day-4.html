Day 4: The fix, and the write-up

At the end of today, every team's review is shorter than it was on Day 2, and every fail from the week is written up so somebody who was not in the room could reproduce it.

Again (5 minutes)

From memory, faster:

  1. Make one mistake in the file. Share. Get it reviewed. Confirm it was caught. Undo it.

Watch (5 to 10 minutes)

  1. Open a second Figma file of your own that is NOT shared with your school email. Copy its link, share it from the demo design brief, and run /design-review. Read the error out loud. Say: the review says the file could not be read and needs to be shared with the teacher's account. It does not ask you to make the file public. That is T21, and it is a test of what the tool refuses to do.
  2. Open the demo team's real review. Pick the top finding. Fix it in Figma, share, run the review. Show the finding gone and the list shorter. Say: the review does not remember. It reads the file fresh every time, so fixing something is the only way to make it go away, and it always works.
  3. Write one bug report on the whiteboard, in the shape from Part 4 of the test page, using the best fail from this week. Say: the last line is a guess and it is allowed to be wrong. A report with a guess in it gets fixed faster.

Say out loud: you did the four things a testing team does. Wrote down what should happen first. Tried the happy path, then tried to break it. Recorded what actually happened. Wrote it so somebody else could see it too.

Do (25 to 30 minutes)

  1. (again) One deliberate mistake, shared, reviewed, caught, undone.
  2. (today) T21: if anyone on the team has a Figma file not shared with the teacher, share its link and have the review run. Expect: could not be read, share it with the teacher's account. If nobody has one, mark it "not run" and say why. That is also a result.
  3. (today) T22: with every deliberate mistake undone, share and get reviewed. Expect: every finding from Day 3 gone, nothing new. Compare line by line with Day 2's review.
  4. (today) Pick the top finding on your real review and fix it in Figma. Share, review, confirm it is gone. If there is time, the next one.
  5. (today) The write-up. For every fail and every weird from the week, one report in the shape from Part 4 of Test the handover: test number, what you did, what it said, what is true, what you think. Put them in the Stuck box on your design brief page, one at a time, so your teacher has them.

Stuck? Ask the helper on your design brief page what a finding means before you call it a fail. If you still think it is wrong after that, it is a fail.

Done when

T21 and T22 recorded, your review shorter than Day 2's, and every fail and weird from the week written up.

After this

Your review is now the finish line for your design. When it says nothing disagrees, your file is the one that gets built for demo day. Keep sharing it as it grows. The review is free and it never gets tired.
