Day 1: Share a file that follows the rules

At the end of today, every team has a Figma file organised the way the handover page says, shared it from their design brief, and seen the box refuse the wrong things before accepting the right one.

Again (5 minutes)

There is no yesterday. Instead, open your design brief and read the list of screens and the component names under the first one. Those names are today.

Watch (5 to 10 minutes)

The teacher does this on the projector, start to finish. Nobody types until it is done.

  1. Open the gallery at /trail-crew, find Handing over your design under the shared pages, and open it. Scroll straight to the checklist at the bottom. Say: every line on this list is something the review checks, so it is the whole rule in eleven lines.
  2. Open your demo Figma file. Show the pages panel: a page called Cover, a page called Card 1 · <title>. Show one frame on the card page named exactly as the brief names the screen. Click one layer and show its name is a component name from the brief. Say: a story and a screen are linked by a name. That is the whole idea.
  3. Press Share in Figma. Add your own school email as a viewer. Say: not "anyone with the link", that makes it public, and it does not need to be. Copy link.
  4. Open your demo team's design brief and scroll to **Share your design**. Paste https://www.google.com into the Figma box and press Share it. Read the refusal out loud. Say: that is a test passing. The app refused something it should refuse.
  5. Paste the real link, type one line in the note, press Share it. Read the "Shared" message. Say: nothing on this page changed, and it will not. The review is a separate thing and it comes tomorrow.
  6. Open your email on the projector and show the message arriving, with the team's name and the line you typed.

Say out loud: today you are testers. Pass, fail, or weird, with one line, for every test. A fail is good news.

Do (25 to 30 minutes)

Pick three jobs: driver, reader, recorder. Swap after ten minutes.

  1. (today) Open Test the handover. Read "How to record a result". The recorder writes T1 to T9 down the side of a page, or in a doc, with room for a word and a line each.
  2. (today) Make the file follow the rules, enough to be reviewed tomorrow: a Cover page; one page named Card N · <title> for one of your build cards, exactly as the card is named; on that page, one frame named exactly as your design brief names one screen; inside it, at least three layers renamed to component names from the brief. Add a second frame named <Screen> / empty. Fifteen minutes. It does not have to be pretty.
  3. (today) Run T1 to T3: find the page from the gallery, find it from your chain, find the share box on the design brief and confirm it is not on the cards page.
  4. (today) Run T4 to T7: the empty share, the Google link, a FigJam link if you have one, https://anvil.works in the Anvil box. Each one should be refused with a message that tells you where to find the real thing. Write down the exact words if the message is confusing.
  5. (today) Share the file with your teacher's school email as a viewer, copy the link, and run T8: the real share. Then T9: your teacher confirms the email arrived and your team is on the queue page.

Stuck? The helper on your design brief page has your brief open and can tell you whether a name matches. It cannot see your Figma file yet. That is tomorrow.

Done when

The recorder can read out nine results, and your teacher has your team's email.

Tomorrow adds

The review. Your teacher reads your file against your documents in front of you, and every finding is either true or a bug.
