Day 3: Break it on purpose

At the end of today, every team has made six deliberate mistakes in their Figma file, one at a time, and seen the review name each one.

Again (5 minutes)

From memory, faster:

  1. Open the review in your chain. Read the one next thing at the top.
  2. Open the Figma file beside it. Point at the thing the finding names.

Watch (5 to 10 minutes)

  1. Open your demo Figma file. Rename the Card 1 · ... page to Screens. Say: I have broken exactly one thing, and I know which.
  2. Share the file again from the design brief, note: "broke the page name on purpose".
  3. Run /design-review and read the findings. Point at the two new lines: the card has no page, and a page is not named after a card. Say: one mistake, two findings, both true. That is a pass.
  4. Undo the rename. Say: tomorrow we check that undoing it makes the findings go away. A test that only checks things appear is half a test.

Say out loud: the point of breaking it is not to break it. It is to know that the review would catch you if you broke it by accident.

Do (25 to 30 minutes)

One mistake at a time. Make it, share, your teacher runs the review, check the finding, write the result, undo it, next. Six rounds, about four minutes each. If the reviews queue up, make the next mistake while you wait and keep a list of which mistakes are in the file at each share.

  1. (again) Open your review and your Figma file side by side.
  2. (today) T15: rename your card page to something else. Expect: that card has no page, and a page is not named after a card.
  3. (today) T16: rename your screen frame to something else. Expect: that screen has no frame, and a frame is not a screen in your brief.
  4. (today) T17: delete your / empty frame. Expect: that screen is named under "no empty state".
  5. (today) T18: rename one component layer to a made-up name with the right prefix, like btn_launch. Expect: that name is not in your brief, and the brief's name is now missing.
  6. (today) T19: add a text layer that says Join 18,000 users. Expect: 18,000 is named as a number not in your documents.
  7. (today) T20: add a text layer that says Lorem ipsum dolor. Expect: named as placeholder text.
  8. For each: pass if the review named it, fail if it did not or named something else, weird if it named it in words you would not have understood without this page. Write the weird ones down word for word.

Stuck? If the review does not catch one, that is not you being stuck. That is the most valuable result of the week. Write it up carefully.

Done when

Six results, T15 to T20, and your file back to how it was before you started.
