    About Blueprint City

    This page is a starter for a Period 3 team whose smart city is still on the drawing board. Their slides are still blank, so there is no city here yet: just an empty lot waiting for ideas.

    How to use the design studio

    1. Pick a location: mountains, coast, desert, plains or a river. The land around the lot changes.
    2. Type a name and a motto for your city. They show on the welcome sign.
    3. Add up to 6 smart features. Tap one on the lot to watch it work and see its Sense, Think and Act.
    4. Check the “My city” card, then play the Sense-Think-Act Challenge and answer the questions below.

    Questions for the designers

    • What should this city be called, and what is its motto?
    • Where should it be — mountains, coast, desert, plains or a river? What is the weather like?
    • What are three problems this city should solve?
    • What is one smart idea for energy? Explain it with Sense, Think, Act.
    • What is one smart idea for getting around, or for keeping people safe?

    Nothing you build here is saved online; answers go to the teacher first.
