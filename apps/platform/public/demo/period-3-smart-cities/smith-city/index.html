About Smith City

City at a glance: Population: “33 million people live here” · Four smart systems: Power, Food, Water, Waste · Every house has one smart speaker, plus a spare in the back.

The designers’ plan

  • Power: “Solar panels get energy, but when it is cloudy the wind turbines do it.”
  • Food: “The farm bots plant food and bring it to the people. The farm bot is very big but not too big.”
  • Water: “A water tower brings water to the people.”
  • Waste: “A trash bot sees trash and throws it away.”
  • People: “33 million people live here.”
  • Spare-speaker idea: “There is one Alexa per house, and when it breaks you already have an extra one in the back.” (In this demo we call it a smart speaker.)

What they drew

  • A farm with crop rows and farm bots, a field of solar panels, and a water tower labelled “water”
  • A little village of 5 houses, trash bots with a trash can, and wind turbines
  • A row of tall city towers, a hotel, a school, a restaurant and Happy Dinner, along an L-shaped road

Added for this demo: the lake next to the water tower, the cars, and Brain Coin Park (the park for the class quiz game). The bots’ names are made up for fun.

Questions for the designer

  • When it's cloudy AND there's no wind, where does Smith City get its power?
  • Solar panels get no sun at night. Do the wind turbines take over at night too, or does the city store energy for later?
  • The farm bot is "very big but not too big." How big is that: as big as a car, a bus, or a house?
  • 33 million people live in Smith City. Where do they all live, with 5 village houses and a row of towers?
  • When a smart speaker breaks, the spare in the back takes over. Who brings the next spare?
  • What else does the smart speaker in each house do? Can it talk to the farm bots or the trash bots?
