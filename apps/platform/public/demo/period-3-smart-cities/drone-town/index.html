About Drone Town

What the student drew

  • A row of smart homes, and each family gets a private drone
  • A drone repair shop
  • A school with papers that write for you
  • A solar field and wind turbines
  • Business buildings, a restaurant where robots cook, and a charging station for electrical cars
  • Self-driving electrical cars, a self-driving bus and a bus stop
  • Smart traffic lights
  • A playground with an elevator to the top of the slide
  • A movie theater with popcorn robots

Questions for the designer

  • Which buildings get power from the solar field, and which from the wind turbines? (The demo guesses with the dotted lines.)
  • At night there's no sun, and some nights there's no wind. Where does Drone Town get power then? Could it store energy for later?
  • What makes the homes "smart", besides the family drones?
  • If papers write themselves, what do students get to do in class while they write?
