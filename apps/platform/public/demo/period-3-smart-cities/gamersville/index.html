About Gamersville

City at a glance: Located in LA, in a town with backup wifi support! · Population: 200,000 gamers · Motto: “Game hard or pay rent”

Big goal: “If you are a gamer you will love this place. You game all day and everything is made by sensors and robots. You don’t have to do any work. Drone delivery is key.”

Problems the team wants to solve

  1. Having to go get food and stop gaming. Who it affects: gamers who play Super Smash Bros all day. Solved by robots.
  2. Wifi not being consistent. Who it affects: gamers who need to be gaming, not getting a new modem. Robots can order a new modem in a second.
  3. Not getting education. Who it affects: kids who have not gone to school because they were too busy gaming. You have to pass an exam to live here.

What the team planned

  • Delivery drones: your phone orders, the app checks your balance, auto-Venmo pays, and the food comes “at the speed of light”
  • Battery and plug-in devices such as smart doors
  • Gaming chairs with a built-in propeller and motor that sense the other chairs
  • Emergency drones that go extra fast when a call comes in or a gaming chair senses something is wrong
  • Robots that dispose of waste with a laser, and robots that bring water
  • One app on your phone, PC or console with a live updating web page. If too many robots or drones are flying, they wait.
  • Map key: energy zone, transportation, safety services, water and parks, homes and schools

The imaginative parts (light-speed food, chairs that fly, laser trash zappers, people turned into light) are the team’s inventions. Each card has a “Real world” line about what exists today.

Challenges they thought about

  • Cost: the rent will be high for the first week; that will take care of cost. Plan: the rent solution.
  • Privacy: nothing is private anymore; that is just how it works. Plan: if there is no privacy online, it will become normal.
  • If it breaks: there are always 300 backup generators. Plan: use the rent money for the generators.

Why live in Gamersville?

  1. If you are a gamer and hate doing other things, then move here and make them automated.
  2. If you prefer watching YouTube, that is OK too; just make sure you are watching videos that have something to do with gaming.
  3. Only 200,000 people live here, so it is not overcrowded.

Sources: the team’s own ideas.

Questions for the designer

  • Nothing can carry food at the speed of light (light has no weight, and a pizza does!). What is the fastest real way you could get food to a gamer?
  • Your deck says drones, doors and consoles run on batteries and plug-in power. Where does that power come from before the 300 backup generators are needed?
  • To live here you have to pass an exam. What is on the exam, and what happens to a kid who hasn't learned it yet?
  • Rent is high for the first week. After that, who pays for all the robots, drones and 300 generators?
  • If nothing is private, someone could see your address or your game accounts. Is there anything you would want to keep private?
  • When too many drones or robots are flying, some have to wait. How many can fly at once, and who goes first: food or emergencies?
