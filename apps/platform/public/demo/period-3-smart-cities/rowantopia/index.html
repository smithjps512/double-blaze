About Rowantopia

City at a glance

Where: plains with rolling hills. Light rainfall with mild temperatures, around the 70s and 80s.

Population: 1 million people and 1,000 robots · Motto: “Build Smarter”

Big goal: “We at Rowantopia want more than anything for people to live happy and healthy. It is our priority to maintain a good environment at all times. That is why we are a smart city.”

Problems the designers want to solve

  1. The electricity bill is very expensive. It affects everyone, because the city needs money for other stuff than just electricity used by robots.
  2. Air pollution. It affects everyone, because the air will make them sick.
  3. The electricity solar panels are not waterproof. It affects the people who use electricity.

What the designers planned

  • Smart cameras and sensors that sense motion, decide when to open doors and interact, solar panels, robots, and streetlights
  • Smart e-cars you start with your ID and talk to, that can take you anywhere
  • A smart ambulance, a smart hospital with an AI robot doctor that scans you, a smart fire department and a smart police department
  • An alert hub: sensors around town send alerts, and a different bell rings for each kind of emergency
  • A water system that senses which house needs water and filters all the water into good drinking water
  • Trash burned far away, so it doesn’t affect the air in Rowantopia

Challenges they thought about

  • Cost: “Our city will be expensive, but we will pay for it with oil money.” Plan: an oil rig that makes a lot of money.
  • Privacy: “We will collect fingerprints that are basically IDs for anywhere you go. We will keep these in a safe in our government building.” Plan: scan everyone’s fingerprints and use those as IDs.
  • If it breaks: “We will have a lot of backup generators that will support us through a struggle.” Plan: backup generators give energy when the power is out.

Why live in Rowantopia?

  • It is a smart city and is very futuristic.
  • We have robots that help everyday life.

Questions for the designer

  • The oil rig pays for the city, but burning oil makes air pollution, and clean air is one of your big goals. How could Rowantopia make money and still keep its air clean?
  • Your solar panels aren't waterproof, and Rowantopia gets light rain. How could you make them waterproof, or keep them dry when it rains?
  • Everyone's fingerprints are kept in a safe at the government building. Who is allowed to open the safe, and how will you keep the fingerprints safe from thieves and hackers?
  • The robots use a lot of electricity, and that makes the bill expensive. How could the 1,000 robots use less power, or make some of their own?
  • At night the solar panels make no power. Where does Rowantopia get electricity after dark? What do the backup generators run on?
  • Your smart cars can take you anywhere in the world. How would one get across an ocean?

Sources: The team’s own ideas.
