About Roseville

City at a glance: in the mountains · Population: 2 million people · Motto: “Smart people, smart city!”

Our big goal: “Because our city will be the best and has smart technology devices.”

Problems the designers want to solve

  1. Landslides (rocks on the road). It affects the health of people in our city.
  2. High electricity bills because of needing heat. It affects the people of our city.
  3. People are littering because they’re too lazy to find a trash can. It affects the wellbeing of the city and the people.

What the designers planned

  • Energy: an automatic home light that notices movement and turns off, dims or turns on by itself
  • Transportation: a bus or taxi that drives itself. Type the address on its number pad and it uses a digital map to take you there
  • Safety: a rain sensor in the street lamps that sends a flood warning to phones and TVs when there is too much rain
  • Waste: a trash robot with a robotic arm on wheels that picks up trash and takes it to the dump or a bigger trash can
  • One connected story: “When the rain light sensor senses a lot of rain, then the bus will drive itself to the nearest open building and wait till the rain is over to keep people safe.”
  • Map key: energy zone, transportation, safety services, water and parks, homes and schools

Challenges they thought about

  • Cost: “For rent, around $100,000.”
  • Privacy: “Our lamp posts could be used as a camera to watch suspicious activity.”
  • If it breaks: “We have a backup generator and it goes for the whole city.”

Why live in Roseville?

  • Our city includes safety measures to keep you safe!
  • Our city has smart devices that can help you with most stuff!

Questions for the designer

  • Your slides don't say where Roseville's power comes from. Sun, wind, a power plant, or water rushing down the mountain? (The demo lets you pick.)
  • Landslides are your problem #1. Which smart idea keeps rocks off the mountain road, or warns drivers in time?
  • How much rain is too much for the rain sensor? Who picks that number, and what should happen for a quick shower?
  • How does the self-driving bus decide who can get on? And what if someone doesn't know the address to type?
  • Your lamp posts could have cameras to watch for suspicious activity. Who gets to see the videos, and how do you keep people's privacy safe?
  • Is the $100,000 for rent the cost of one home, or of all of Roseville's smart technology? Where does the money come from?
