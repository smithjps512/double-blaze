    About Smithsburg

    The perfect place to slow your pace. Period 3 students designed this smart city on paper and in a slide deck.

    City at a glance

    • Where: a coastal city, with a beach area full of stores and restaurants.
    • Population: 67,000,000 (as written in the plan).

    Problems Smithsburg is solving

    1. Speeding in school zones. Speed cameras alert the police, who send a ticket. This affects students, crazy drivers, normal drivers and people walking.
    2. Flooding, because the city is by the ocean.
    3. Overpriced gas. They want to make it cheaper.

    Challenges they thought about

    • Cost: recycle things to make them brand new, so it's easier to afford.
    • Privacy: everyone has their own privacy; the internet won't leak anything.
    • If it breaks: everyone has an extra plug-free internet box.

    Why live here?

    • It saves your money with smart devices.
    • It's very safe, with lots of cameras and locks.
    • Great privacy.

    A connected city

    "When a traffic light senses a car, it can turn on all the lights in that area and open doors." Try it: tap the traffic light, then Connected: car at night.

    What's on the map

    • A self-driving car (like Waymo) on the road
    • A sensor traffic light and a sensor wall to prevent getting run over
    • A food delivery robot carrying a burger
    • An electric train on a curved track
    • A clock tower restaurant and a mall with a 1st and 2nd floor
    • A sensor trash can, robots, and a house with a smart door
    • A school-zone speed camera and a police RV parked nearby
    • Motion-sensor ("censored") lights along the sidewalk
    • A flood sensor by the ocean, a safety app that looks like a game, and a water saver for the AI center

    Questions for the designers

    • Your train is electric — where does the city's electricity come from? Sun, wind, or something else?
    • What jobs do the robots do around Smithsburg?
    • When the sensor wall pops up, how does it know when it's safe to go back down so people can cross?
    • How will Smithsburg stop the ocean from flooding the city?
    • How would smart technology make gas cheaper?
    • Smithsburg has 67 million people, more than most countries. Where do they all fit by the beach?
