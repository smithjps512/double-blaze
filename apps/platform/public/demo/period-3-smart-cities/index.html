Period 3 Smart Cities

Four smart cities drawn by the sixth grade 3rd period class, turned into living maps. Tap anything to watch it work, change the sun and wind to power the city, and find every feature the designer thought of.

Connection Center City Energy

A big poster city

A Solar Stack and windmills feed the Connection Center, which controls everything. Streets store sunlight for the night, houses move, and robots take your order.

Drone Town Energy

A top-down city map

A solar field and wind turbines, a private drone for every family, self-driving cars and buses, robot cooks, popcorn robots, and a playground with an elevator up to the slide.

Sensor Street

A street grid

Motion sensors trigger the crosswalks and turn the traffic lights red, a restaurant sign flips to "closed" by itself, and a door opens when you step on a pressure plate.

Clock Tower Square

A city street

A self-driving car, a sensor wall that keeps people from getting run over, a food delivery robot, an electric train, a clock tower restaurant, and a sensor trash can.

These demos were built from drawings. Each one has a list of questions for its designer in the About panel. Nothing you do here is saved.
