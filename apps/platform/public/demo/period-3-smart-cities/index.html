Period 3 Smart Cities

Nine smart cities designed by the sixth grade 3rd period class, from their drawings and slide decks, turned into living maps. Tap anything to watch it work, play each city's games for the class leaderboard, and answer the questions the designers left open.

Every city has: a map where everything works · games with a class leaderboard (initials only) · questions for the designers.

Connection Center City Keep the power on

A big poster city

A Solar Stack and windmills feed the Connection Center, which controls everything. Streets store sunlight for the night, houses move, and robots take your order.

Solar City City Dispatcher

“Stay solar!” · on the coast

Drones for every family, cars you talk to, police and ambulances, trash robots and a river. Run the city's data hub and keep the drones from crashing.

Romanville Safety missions

“SPQR” · Southwest Virginia

Motion sensors trigger the crosswalks, a sign flips to closed by itself, and a door opens when you step on a pressure plate. Be the sensor!

Smithsburg Safety missions

“The perfect place to slow your pace” · by the beach

A self-driving car, a sensor wall, a delivery robot, an electric train, school-zone speed cameras and a safety app that looks like a game.

Roseville Storm Watch

“Smart people, smart city!” · in the mountains

Rain sensors in the street lamps send flood warnings, self-driving buses find shelter, and sensors watch the mountain road for landslides.

Gamersville Drone Rush

“Game hard or pay rent” · Los Angeles

Food drones so gamers never stop playing, backup wifi, a modem robot, emergency drones and gaming chairs that drive themselves.

Smart Yale City Storm Shelter

“Light and truth” · flat, by the coast

Solar panels that follow the sun, a scouting drone, storm shelters and smart sewers. Get everyone safe before the hurricane.

Rowantopia Rescue Bells

“Build Smarter” · plains and rolling hills

A robot doctor, smart fire, police and ambulance stations, water sent to the house that needs it, and 1,000 helper robots.

Blueprint City Design studio

Still on the drawing board

This team's city is still being designed. Build a draft city and play the Sense-Think-Act Challenge to help them dream it up.

Built from the students’ own drawings and slide decks; their words are quoted on every card. No student names are shown. Answers to designer questions appear only after the teacher approves them. Scores go on a class leaderboard under initials; nothing else is saved.
