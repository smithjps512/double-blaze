🕸️ About Thomasville

Thomasville is a web-slinging smart city. Every building is connected by webs, and the city's smart sensors work like spider-sense.

How to use it

  • 🏙️ City: tap a building. Your hero swings there and shows how it works.
  • 🕸️ Web Builder: tap a dot, then another dot, to spin a strand. Save your best webs.
  • ⭐ Missions: pick a web, put it in the right spot, and help the city.
  • 🎨 Design my city: pick suit colors, a hero name, the sky and new buildings.
  • 💬 My ideas: tap pictures to answer, then send them to your teacher.

Questions for the designer

    Nothing is saved online except ideas you send to your teacher.
