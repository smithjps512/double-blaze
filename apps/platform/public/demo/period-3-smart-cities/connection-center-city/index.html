About Connection Center City

A Period 3 student made a big poster out of taped-together pages. The title says the whole city is "powered by connection center." The student drew:

  • a Solar Stack of tall panel towers that send sun energy to the Connection Center
  • the Smart Connection Center, run by people, that controls everything
  • Smart Streets that store sunlight for the night
  • a Spa, a Gym with a 5-step check, and a Pool
  • Windmills and an Energy Plant
  • Smart Houses that can move
  • a Smart Restaurant (drawn as a Chipotle) with sensor doors and robots
  • a Medical Center with a smart elevator, sensor doors and smart air conditioning
  • WiFi Towers that pass signals to the Connection Center

Questions for the designer

  • The Connection Center is "powered by power plant." Is that the Energy Plant next to the windmills, or a different one?
  • How do the Smart Houses move? Do they walk, roll, or fly, and where do they go?
  • At night the Solar Stack gets no sun. Does the city run on wind then, or does it save sunlight like the streets do?
  • Could the people on the gym machines make electricity for the city, instead of only using it?
