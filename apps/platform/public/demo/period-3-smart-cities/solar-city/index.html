About Solar City

City at a glance: a coastal city that is warm and humid · Population: “43 billion people” · Motto: “Stay solar!”

“Our city is smart because we have drones, self driving cars and so much more. People should come here so they can live in a city that is good for the environment.”

Problems the designers want to solve

  1. Many traffic jams: people can’t get where they need to go fast.
  2. Too many drones at a time: drones crash when delivering.
  3. Not enough money to pay the tax for all the drones: the government has to pay.

What the designers planned

  • Solar panels and wind turbines that power the internet and the lights in houses
  • Self-driving cars and buses: you say where you are going and they take you there
  • Lots of ambulances and police cars (“People are saved 30 times a day.”)
  • Trash-picking robots that don’t run on oil, sent by an alert app to a recycling bin
  • Family drones, drone repair, and drone traffic control
  • Schools and homes, a river, smart traffic lights, business buildings, a robot restaurant, a charging station
  • A playground with an elevator to the top of the slide, and movies with “popcorn next to all the theater chairs”

Challenges they thought about

  • Cost: all the buildings and drones cost a lot. Plan: don’t tax too high.
  • Privacy: don’t share important info.
  • If it breaks: fix it right away when someone reports it.

Questions for the designer

  • Which buildings get power from the solar field, and which from the wind turbines? (The demo guesses with the dotted lines.)
  • At night there's no sun, and some nights there's no wind. Where does Solar City get power then? Could it store energy for later?
  • Solar City has 43 billion people, but all of Earth has about 8 billion. Where do all those people live?
  • How many drones can fly at the same time before it's "too many"? How does the city decide?
  • If taxes stay low, who pays for all the drones and robots?
  • If papers write themselves, what do students get to do in class while they write?
