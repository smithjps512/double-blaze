Home

Gaming PCs are built for people with money. A student who saves up for months still ends up choosing between a machine that is too expensive and one that will not run the games they actually play, and nobody explains which of the two they are buying. PC BROS is for kids like us: pick your own parts, watch the price add up as you go, and get a machine that is exactly the one you designed, hand built, with your name on it if you want it there.

PC BROS is a custom PC shop for students and young people. You choose your own parts on our site, the price updates as you pick each one, and when you are happy you place the order and we build it by hand. Every machine is built to that exact order, with an engraving on the metal if you want one, so what turns up is yours rather than whatever was in the warehouse. You can watch your order's progress from ordered to built to shipped. And if you would rather do it in person, our store is a real place you can walk into and build it on the bench with us.
