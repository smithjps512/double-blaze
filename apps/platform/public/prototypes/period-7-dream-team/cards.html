Card 1: Build my own PC

Your story. As a kid who games, I want to choose the parts for my own PC, so that I get a machine I like at a price I can afford.

(Your story said "As a PC company I want to make money so that I can be rich." Every one of your acceptance criteria underneath it is something the company does. A user story is written from the point of view of the person using the app. Your customer is a kid who wants a good PC cheaply, and that is who this rewrite is for. Notice that the app you would build for the customer is a completely different app from the one you would build for the company.)
