A note for the whole team

Your app is the smallest in the period and that is good. Four features you can actually finish beats seventeen you cannot start. Get all four working, then add the engraving box, then add whatever you want. Finishing is a skill and not many teams get to practise it.

Your story is wrong?

That happens, and noticing it is good. Rewrite it here and it goes to your teacher. Nothing changes until he approves it. When he does, your story and your prototype update by themselves.
