Math Bros Inc. Everyone

Home

To teach people math so they can get smarter.

An app that teaches math. Different courses on different subjects of math, for example addition and subtraction, trigonometry, 2 digit multiplication, factoring any kind of polynomial of higher degrees, and graph theory, with videos and guides on how to do it.

Video

A video in the course to help visual learners understand math, so it teaches people math and they get smarter.

Watch a lesson video in a courseSo that I can see the material explained before I try itFor: student

Each course has a lesson with a name and a length

Play and pause it

Switch to 2x speed

Skip to a part of the lesson by tapping the timeline

Quiz

A quiz at the end of the course, so it helps you get more familiar with the mathematical subjects and get more knowledge.

Take a quiz at the end of a courseSo that I can see if I learned the materialFor: student

The quiz has five questions from that course

At the end it shows how many were right and how many wrong

A quiz score of 4 or 5 earns 50 points

Placement test

It sorts users based on skill level, so the experience is not too complicated or too easy.

Take a placement testSo that the questions I get are not too hard or too easyFor: new student

The test has ten questions that get harder as they go

After each answer I am told whether it was right

At the end I see how many I got right and how many wrong

Retake the test and my level changes if my score does

AI assistant

You put in a mathematical equation and it gives the answer, like a calculator for everything. It also gives you the steps to solve it, so you learn more about problems you do not know.

Type it in and get the answer with the stepsSo that I learn how to do it and not just what the answer isFor: student who is stuck on a problem

The assistant shows the answer and the steps to get there

If it cannot solve what I typed, it says so and suggests what it can do

Secret age groups

After the placement test, users get assigned to an age group. They do not get told. This way everyone gets a personalized experience with the appropriate level of challenge.

The math to fit my skill level without being told what group I am inSo that nobody feels labelledFor: student

Getting three practice questions right in a row moves me up a level

Getting two wrong in a row moves me down a level

Practise a subject with questions at my levelSo that I get better at it and earn pointsFor: student

There are six subjects: Algebra, Geometry, Calculus, Statistics, Trigonometry and Number Theory

Each question is typed or multiple choice and I am told right away if I was right

A right answer is worth 10 points

A typed answer is not marked wrong for capital letters or spaces

The screen shows how many I got right and wrong this session

Leaderboard

A leaderboard for the most XP earned, because competition encourages players to keep playing.

See who has the most pointsSo that I have a reason to keep practisingFor: student

The leaderboard lists everyone from most points to least

My row is highlighted and shows my rank

Points on the leaderboard are the same points I earned in practice, quizzes and races

The board tells me how many points I need to move up one place

Racing game against AI

A racing game against an AI slightly more advanced than your level, so students work harder to beat it.

Race a bot at math questionsSo that I work harder to beat itFor: student who is bored of plain practice

The bot moves forward on its own, faster on harder difficulties

Move forward one space for every right answer, and a wrong answer says so

First to the finish wins, and beating the bot earns 100 points

Dictionary

A dictionary for many mathematical subjects and theorems, a handy reference for students confused about a word.

Look it upSo that I can carry on with the problemFor: student who does not understand a word in a problem

The dictionary is in alphabetical order

Each word has a plain definition and an example

A word that is not there says so and offers the closest match

Secret mode

Accessed by pressing a key combination. A prank where calculations are off by one, so 1 plus 1 is 3. No benefits, just funny.

Pick a usernameSo that my points and my place on the leaderboard are mineFor: student

Sign in asks for a username and nothing else

An empty username is refused with a message that says so

Change my username later and the leaderboard shows the new one

A hidden mode where calculations are off by oneSo that I can prank a friendFor: student who likes a joke

Tapping the logo five times turns secret mode on and shows a small banner

In secret mode the AI assistant's answers are one too big

Tapping the logo five more times turns it off

Secret mode never changes points or the leaderboard
