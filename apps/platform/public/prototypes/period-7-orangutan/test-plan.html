Test plan: Math Bros Inc. by Team Orangutan

Nobody wrote this page. It was worked out from your own user stories by following three rules, and you could follow the same three rules on paper.

  1. Every Given/When/Then scenario is already a test. Given is the setup, When is what somebody does, Then is what should happen. Nothing to invent.
  2. Every acceptance criterion becomes a test that checks it. It gives you what has to be true, and leaves you to work out what you would do.
  3. Every rule that refuses something gets a second test that tries to do the refused thing, because a rule you have not broken on purpose is a rule you do not know works.

And one refusal: a criterion nobody could check by looking at the app does not get a test. It gets a note saying so, because a made up test for it would be worse than nothing.
