Home

To service mountain biking communities to help them find trails and materials easier.

TrailRider is an app with a mountain bike shop for supplies, a map with a bunch of trails, music suggestions, and health and training advice. The shop is similar to Facebook Marketplace, where people post listings for bikes, helmets, parts and so on. The map is full of trails with descriptions of the trails made by bikers, along with a difficulty level. There is an event page where bikers can find rides and events. The shop allows bikers to avoid the hassle of searching on Google. The practice advice makes bikers practice better. The map lets bikers choose good trails, and the event page allows mountain bikers to find people like them and participate in fun races.

Shop

A shop similar to Facebook Marketplace where riders can sell biking related stuff like bike gloves, and people can buy them by messaging the sellers on the built in messaging system.
