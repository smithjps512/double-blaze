Home

An app that combines Wikipedia, YouTube, TikTok and other elements. The app is an informational and social media app.

This app combines the elements of Wikipedia, YouTube, TikTok, Snap and Discord. You read informative topics, and you can also watch videos, text and call. Wikipedia becomes Encyclopedia: topics you can read, with sources, photos and videos to explain them, possibly with a quiz feature. YouTube becomes Media: long form videos and content for engagement. TikTok becomes Media Shorts: short form videos and content. Snap is merged with Discord into Chats: group, public or private, message chat and call.
