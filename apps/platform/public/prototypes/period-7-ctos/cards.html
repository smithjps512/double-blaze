Your story is wrong?

That happens, and noticing it is good. Rewrite it here and it goes to your teacher. Nothing changes until he approves it. When he does, your story and your prototype update by themselves.

Stuck?

I will tell you which page and which pattern answers it. I will not write the code, because finding it is what makes you able to do it next time.

Something broken is different. Paste what Anvil is telling you, or the code you have, and I will explain what it means and show you the fix. Pick the story it is for and paste your code, and I will write the whole thing with your own names in it.

Every question is logged for your teacher, with your team name and nothing else.

Figma is different again: nothing about it is hidden in your documents, so I can just answer. How do I start? How do I make it look like a real app? How do I make a button do something? How do I get this into Anvil?
