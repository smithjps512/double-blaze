What next
Cuisinely Everyone

Home

To show all restaurants in the area, and the menu.

The app will allow you to view the schedules and menus of each restaurant, as well as the ratings and amount of calories in each food item. Users will be able to see all the reviews, ratings, and schedules of the local, smaller restaurants. They will also have an accessible menu with all the calories and other important information. This app will also have the ability to deliver.

Delivery

Needed. Order from a restaurant and have it delivered to your address.

Sign up with just my emailSo that I can order without filling in a form firstFor: somebody hungry

Cuisinely is free and sign up asks for an email and nothing else

An empty or badly formed email is refused with a message that says so

My delivery address is asked for the first time I order, not at sign up

After signing up I land on the map

Choose items from one restaurant and have them delivered to my addressSo that I can eat without going outFor: somebody ordering food

The order shows every item, how many, and the total

Ordering for delivery with no address is refused with a message that says why

Items from two restaurants cannot be in one order, and the app says so

After ordering I am told it worked and the order is in My Orders
