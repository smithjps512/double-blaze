Classic Cars: build cards

Team: Classic Cars.

One card per story. Next stop: build-architecture.md. After that: docs/build/anvil-patterns.md.

Read this first. Three of these four cards were drafted with your teacher from what you said you liked about cars, so the first job is to read them and change what is wrong. They are a starting point, not a verdict. The quiz card is the story you wrote yourselves.

Card 1: Car gallery

Your story. As somebody who likes cars, I want to look through cool cars and their stats, so that I can see which ones are fastest and why.

Done when:

Build it: Architecture, Feature 1. Build this one first. It is the quickest win in the whole app.

Your job before you code: pick the cars. Five is plenty to start. They are your app's personality, so choose ones you actually think are cool.

Card 2: Parts library

Your story. As somebody who likes cars but does not know how they work, I want to read what each part does, so that I understand what is happening under the hood.

Done when:

Build it: Architecture, Feature 2.

Your job before you code: write the part pages. Engine, turbo, exhaust, brakes, tires is a good five. Three or four sentences each, in words you would actually use. The two sites you picked out both do exactly this, so look at how they explain things.

Card 3: Horsepower builder

Your story. As somebody who wants a faster car, I want to pick upgrades and watch the horsepower change, so that I can see what actually makes a car more powerful.

Done when:

Build it: Architecture, Feature 3.

This is the one you asked for. You told your teacher you like "what you can do to give the car more horsepower", and this is that, on a screen.

Card 4: Quiz

Your story. As a user, I want to take a quiz, so that I can test my knowledge about the cars and parts I learned about.

(This is your own story, the one you handed in, tidied up. It was written in the first person using the author's own name as the user type, and a story names a kind of person rather than one particular person, so it is now "As a user". Everything else is yours.)

Done when:

Build it: Architecture, Feature 4. Build it last, not because it is unimportant but because it is the hardest, and the other three teach you everything it needs.

A note for the whole team

Your app has no stubbed features. Every other team in the class has parts of their plan parked for later because they cannot be built in the time we have. You do not, because the product you picked is made of the things Anvil is actually good at.

That is worth knowing about yourselves: you chose a buildable idea. Plenty of professional teams do not.
