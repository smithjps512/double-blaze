House Point Tracker Everyone

Home

Track the points of all houses at BMS. It helps track the number of points each house has in real time so students can check it.

When you open the app you see the current point tallies of each house. If you click on a house, it shows further information about the house including the students in it. The app has many features including instant point updates, scheduled points by teachers, and the daily amount given and gained by each teacher, student and house.
