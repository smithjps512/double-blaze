Bus Buddy Everyone

Home

Students at our school wait outside for buses that are late, early, or already gone, and nobody tells them which. Parents call the front office to ask where the bus is, and the office cannot answer either. Bus Buddy tells everybody the same thing at the same time so nobody is standing in the rain guessing.

Bus Buddy is a phone app that shows where school buses are and when they will arrive. Students set their bus number and their stop, and the app shows a countdown. Drivers can report a delay in two taps, and every rider on that route sees it immediately. The front office gets a board with every route on it.
