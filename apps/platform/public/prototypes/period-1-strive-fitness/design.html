Strive Fitness: design brief

Everything on this page came out of your own build architecture. Nobody typed it for you, and nobody will keep it up to date by hand: change the architecture and this page changes with it.

That is the point. Your builders have already agreed on the name of every button and every label in Strive Fitness, by BMS Crew. If your Figma layers use those names, the person writing the code can look at your design and find their own component in it. If they do not, they are guessing.

The rules for how to set the file up are on Designing for Anvil. This page is only your team's part.

Draw the bad day too

Every screen has three states and designers almost always draw one of them. The happy one. Your builder then has to invent the other two while writing code, at speed, and it shows.

Empty. Nothing has been added yet.

You have a repeating list on History and Chatroom. On the first day of using this app, that list has nothing in it. What does the screen say? "Nothing here yet" is a design decision, and it is yours.

Wrong. Somebody typed something the app will not accept.

Your architecture already has an error label: lbl_error on LogActivity. It starts invisible and appears when something is refused. Right now nobody has designed what it looks like. Red text? A box? Where on the screen? Decide it, because otherwise it will be default grey in the middle of nowhere.

Full. Somebody has used this for a month.

Long names, a hundred rows, a review that is a paragraph rather than four words. Designs break at the edges, and the edges are where real people live.

The pieces you are working with

These are the components your team is actually using. Anvil gives you a fixed set of them, which is a real constraint on the design and not a small one. Figma will happily let you draw something nobody on your team can build.

ComponentYou can changeYou cannot
ButtonThe text, the icon, the text colour, the background colour, and the size.Give it a custom shape, a gradient, or a hover animation.
DropDownThe list of choices and the width.Style the open list, or put pictures in the choices.
LabelThe words, size, colour, bold or italic, alignment, and an icon beside it.Make it a box with a border and a background of its own.
TextBoxThe placeholder text, the width, and whether it hides what is typed.Put an icon or a button inside the box itself.
RepeatingPanelEverything inside the one row.Make row three look different from row one.

Before you hand it over

Then show it to whoever is writing the code and ask them one question: can you find every one of your components in this? If the answer is no, you have found the gap before it cost anybody a period of work, which is the entire job.

Ask about your design

Good ones: can Anvil do a card with a shadow? Does my design match what my team is building? What should the empty state say? I have your screens and your component names in front of me.
