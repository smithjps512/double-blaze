    Name the layers

    Double-click each layer's name in the left panel and give it the name from the table. Ten seconds a layer.

    Three reasons, and the third surprises people:

    1. Your builder can find their component in your design.
    2. You can find things yourself when there are sixty layers.
    3. Smart animate, later, works by matching layers with identical names in two frames. Named layers get smooth animation for free. Rectangle 41 gets nothing.

    Make the buttons look like buttons

    A button needs to look pressable, or nobody will press it when you test.

    Draw a rectangle, give it a rounded corner (the corner radius box in the Design panel), put the text on it, and give it one colour that appears almost nowhere else on the screen. That colour is your accent. Everything else stays black, white and grey. This one rule is most of what "professional" means.

    Click the colour swatch and copy its hex code (six characters after a #). Write it down. Hex codes are the only form a colour can cross into Anvil in.

    Delete what the card never asked for
    
    
    A search bar. A profile picture. Three tabs. A footer with a privacy policy. If it is not on the card, it is not in Strive Fitness, and your builder will either build it (and it will do nothing) or skip it (and the design will not match). Delete it.
    

    Sometimes you look at a thing the tool added and think, oh, we do need that. Good. That is a finding. Propose it on your cards page so it goes on the story first, and then keep it. Adding to the story is how a thing gets into the app. Adding to the screen on its own is how a design and an app drift apart.

    Every screen has three states and designers almost always draw one of them: the good one. Your builder then invents the other two while writing code, at speed, and it shows.
    

    Select a frame, press Ctrl/Cmd + D to duplicate it, and name the copy with a slash and the state:

    Empty is what the screen says when nothing has been added yet. On the first day Strive Fitness is real, that is the only state anybody sees. History and Chatroom have a repeating list, so the empty state is a real design decision: what does the list say with nothing in it?
    

    Error is the app refusing something. LogActivity and Run and Subscribe and Coach have an lbl_error that starts invisible. Decide what it looks like when it appears: red text, a box, where on the screen. Otherwise it is default grey in the middle of nowhere.

    Test it on somebody who is not you
    
    
    This is the actual point of the whole exercise, and most teams skip it.
    

    Find somebody who has not seen your design. Do not explain it. Say one sentence, something like the job in your first card: I want to sign up. Then say nothing at all and watch what they do.
    

    Where they hesitate is where your design is wrong. Not where they are stupid. Where your design is wrong. It is uncomfortable the first time and it is the most useful ten minutes of the project.
