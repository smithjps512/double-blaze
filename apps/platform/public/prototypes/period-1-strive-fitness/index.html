Strive Fitness Everyone

Home

An app that encourages you to be active, while doing everyday activities. Tell you the weather so you can know if there are good conditions for physical activities. Telling you about nearby areas with the nutrition your body needs after being active.

Signup/login

The app will let you sign up for the app. The user will sign up through Google, email, or Apple. Using Apple, Google, or email will make signup less challenging because you have the choice between them.

Sign up with my email and a PINSo that my activity is saved to me and nobody elseFor: new user

Sign up asks for an email and a username, and the username must be unique

The password is a 4 digit PIN, and anything else is refused with a message that says so

A confirmation code is sent, and in this demo it appears on an Email Inbox screen

Signing in again later needs only my email and my PIN

A chat room to see who has done the most physical activity

The app will be able to have a chat room where you can look at other people's activity and post what you have been doing. It will allow users to be more social and encourage them to do more activities.

Log an activity I did without the app and see everything I have doneSo that my history is completeFor: user

Log an activity asks for what I did, how far in miles, and how many minutes

Miles and minutes must be numbers, and an empty or non number entry is refused

History lists every run and activity, newest first, with distance and time

History shows my totals: activities, miles and minutes

Every logged activity earns credits

Chat with my friends on the app and share what I didSo that we encourage each otherFor: consumer

Ask a coach questions during my workoutsSo that I do them rightFor: fitness user

If the question is not in the table, the coach says so and lists what it can help with

The coach never gives medical advice and says to tell an adult about pain

A credit system

The app will have a credit system. You can earn credits by doing activity and you can spend credits on cooler customization options. It would make users want to do more things so they can customize their space.

Earn credits by being active and spend them on backgroundsSo that my app feels like mineFor: fitness person

Every logged activity or saved run earns credits: 10 per activity plus 5 per mile

My credits show on the home screen

The Edit section lists background colours; some are free and some cost credits

Buying a background takes the credits and applies it everywhere in the app

A background I cannot afford says how many more credits I need

Food suggestions

Our app will be able to give suggestions for healthy nutritious food suggested by your peers. Will have moderation and a report button for users to alert of bad options. It allows users to have healthier foods without having to find any online, so after their activities they can have nutritious food.

Healthy food ideas suggested by other users nearbySo that I refuel without searching onlineFor: someone who has just been active

Food shows suggestions with the place, what to get, and who suggested it

Suggestions I added show my username

Weather app

To see if outside is good for the activities. Because if there is a category 5 tornado, if you check the weather app you won't die. So we can save lives and get our app promoted.

The app to check the weather and plan my weekSo that I know whether to be active inside or outsideFor: app user

Good weather gives an outdoor plan, bad weather an indoor one, and a storm says stay in

The weekly plan shows seven days with an activity each

Accept the plan, reroll a new one, or make my own

A plan I accepted shows on the home screen as today's activity

Start a run and watch my stats liveSo that I can see how I am doing and save the run when I finishFor: runner

The screen shows distance in km and mi, time, average pace and calories

Calories are estimated at 60 per kilometre and the screen says so

Distance is simulated in this demo and the screen says so, because a web page cannot promise a phone's GPS
