Home

To rearrange your room without having to actually move the furniture. The only work you have to do is scan the room.

You scan your room using the app, then press a button to upload it. The app creates a 3D visual of the room, the furniture becomes movable, and you can rearrange the room however you want without actually moving anything. It can do things that are very hard to undo in real life, for example changing the backsplash in your kitchen.
