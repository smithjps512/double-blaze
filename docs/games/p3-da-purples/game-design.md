# build on

Team: da purples

Period 3. Genre: Survival.

## Pitch

We are making a game where you get lost at sea and you land in the middle of nowhere and you have to build it and you can tread with neighboring towns and you can mine kinda like the survival game the people made They die if they don't have a lot of food or die of animals if they die it will show how far they got.They can get people it will make them feel proud and good but it will still be a.

## The feeling

Proud and useful.

## Players

**builders:** To make there own world and rule it.

**colonist:** To colonize and trade/develop.

**fighter:** To fight other things.

## Core loop

get materials → build → eat/sleep → craft → find more people → again

**Win:** You build the best city and capitalize.

**Lose:** You die of hunger, sleep, or your city dies.

## World

**Hero:** Avery.

**Goal:** To build a great city.

**Obstacle:** Food, sleep, water, people, and disease.

**Setting:** A foreign place.

You were walking down the bay as a huge wave sweeps you away, you wake up on this island in the middle of nowhere, you decided to build a city to survive.

## Mechanics

**jump** (space key): Space bar is a jump up 2.

**go around** (arrow keys): If you press a arrow you go that way.

**build** (butten 1): If you press 1 it puts down the matirials.

**Inventory** (butten 9): It goes to your Inventory in till you press it again in 2.
