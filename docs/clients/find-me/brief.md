## Purpose

An app that helps animal shelters match people who want to adopt with the
animals that are available, using many different criteria. Beyond adoption, it
should be the modern, everyday home for local pet lovers: a place to share
photos and stories about their own pets and, most importantly, about the pets
waiting for a home at local shelters.

## The problem

Most shelters do not do a great job of promoting themselves. Their adoptable
animals are hard to find, hard to compare, and easy to forget. Owners who lose
a pet have nowhere to turn except public social media, where a conversation
with a stranger who found their dog happens in front of everyone. New adopters
leave the shelter with a pet and very little ongoing guidance.

## Who will use it

| User | What they need |
| :---- | :---- |
| **Animal shelter staff** | A fast, easy way to add their animals and the details that matter, so the matching can connect each animal to the right adopters. It must not create a lot of extra work. |
| **New adopters** | Sign up, describe their home, lifestyle, and what they want in a pet, and see the animals that match. |
| **Returning adopters** | People who adopted through the app before and now want another pet, have lost their pet and need help finding it, or need help with the pet they adopted. |
| **Pet owners** | People who already have a pet and want to connect with shelters, vets, and other owners. |
| **Veterinarians** | Share tips and information, answer questions, schedule appointments, and give updates on a pet's treatment. |
| **Emergency veterinarians** | Offer 24 hour access to care nearby. Set their availability and show current wait times. |
| **Community supporters** | Volunteers, foundations, and support groups for animal safety and care that are not shelters. |

## What the app does

| Feature | Description | Benefit |
| :---- | :---- | :---- |
| Local adoptable pets | See the available pets at nearby shelters, with photos and bios. | Shelters get modern promotion; adopters find pets easily. |
| Matching | Adopters enter their needs and preferences; the app matches them to animals that fit. | Better matches, fewer returns. |
| Easy shelter onboarding | Shelters provide pets through a simple tool or an integration with the photos and bios they already have. | No extra work for shelters. |
| Adoption QR code | When someone adopts, the shelter gives them a QR code unique to that shelter. They scan it and choose the pet they adopted. | Connects every adoption back to the app. |
| Care plan | Scanning the QR code gives the new owner a complete care plan for that pet, with reminders for feeding, water, vet visits, and more. | Healthier pets, a reason to keep using the app. |
| Lost pets | Owners post a lost pet in the app first, then share it to social media. People who find the pet or want to help talk to the owner directly in the app, safely and privately. Shelters and vets are notified automatically, including help with microchip lookups. | Faster, safer reunions. |
| Ask a Vet | General tips, an AI assistant for simple questions about a pet, appointment scheduling, and follow up on treatment. | Help between visits. |
| Emergency vet | 24 hour access to a nearby emergency vet, with live availability and wait times. | Fast care when it matters most. |
| Community and sharing | Share photos and stories of your own pets and of shelter pets. | Daily use; shelter pets get seen. |
| Giving | Pet food drives and other drives that support owners and shelters. | The philanthropic heart of the app. |
| Learning game | A game about pets, pet care, and where your local shelters are. It features real shelter pets that are available. Players can win prizes like 25% off a pet toy, provided by advertisers. | Gets young people involved; funds prizes through advertisers. |

## How it launches

We start with one or two local shelters as partners who allow us to show their
available pets. We work with them to give extra value to people who adopt
through the app.

## What is critical to quality

- **Real and local.** This is for real people and real shelters near us, not a
  demo.
- **No extra work for shelters.** If it is a burden, shelters will not use it.
- **Safe and private.** Conversations about lost pets and adoptions happen in
  the app, not in public.
- **Modern and fun.** Something people want to open every day.
