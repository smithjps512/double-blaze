# The Homework Helper

Team: techies

Period 3. For: Blacksburg Middle School.

## Purpose

This app helps struggling students better understand their classwork and homework. They can take a screenshot and upload it to the app to get step by step help with a question.

## Who are the users

**Students:** It helps kids learn more about their work.

**Teachers:** They can work on other work, leading to less stress at school.

## Product description

This app can help students when they are struggling on a question.

## Features

**Homework Helper:** A student can get help with their work.

**Parent helper:** A parent can help their children with their work without having to relearn the new style of work.
