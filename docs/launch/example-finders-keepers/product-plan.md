# Finders Keepers

Team: Example Team

For: Our school. This is the worked example from the slides, not a real team.

## Purpose

Students lose jackets, water bottles and headphones every week, and most of it gets donated before anyone finds it. Because of Finders Keepers, kids get their things back and families stop buying the same jacket twice.

## Who are the users

**Students:** They want their stuff back fast, without digging through a giant pile in front of everybody.

**Front office staff:** They want the pile gone and fewer interruptions at the desk.

**Parents:** They do not want to pay for the same thing twice.

**Custodians:** They find most of the lost things and want a quick place to drop them off.

## Product description

A lost and found you can search from your phone, with a photo of everything that gets turned in.

## Features

**Photo search:** A student can look through photos of everything turned in this week.

**Post a found item:** A front office staff can snap a photo of a found item and post it in under a minute.

**Name alert:** A parent can get a message when something with their kid's name on it is turned in.

**Drop-off spots:** A custodian can see the closest drop-off bin on their floor.
