# Survival

Team: The Smiths

Period 3. For: the USA.

## Purpose

To help kids understand how to make stuff and survive.

## Who are the users

**Students:** To help them understand the world in the past, and how to make present day items.

## Product description

A game where you have to survive out in the wild with no minerals, with hunger, thirst and health. There are towns, skaters and wild animals, and you can make anything in the world, but only if you have the recipe.

## Features

**Survival:** A student can have fun surviving in the past, but with modern day stuff.
