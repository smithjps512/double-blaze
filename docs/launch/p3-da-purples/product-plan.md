# Find your way

Team: Da purples

Period 3. For: new kids at BMS.

## Purpose

Some new kids have a problem with getting to the right class. Because of Find your way, they can get there on time.

## Who are the users

**New student:** They can get to class on time and find it easily.

**Teachers:** Now their students will get to class on time and won't be marked absent.

## Product description

A map to help you navigate around the school.

## Features

**School navigation:** A new student can easily find their class and not be late.

**Packed hallways:** A student can know how crowded it is in the hallways and how much time they have before the bell rings.
