# Find me

Team: Pickle bombs

Period 3. For: Blacksburg and Christiansburg, VA.

## Purpose

Our purpose is that every animal can find a home, and to prevent overcrowding at animal shelters.

## Who are the users

**Animal shelter workers:** It matters because it can prevent stressful situations where there is not enough space for animals.

**People looking for pets:** They want to find the right pet for their house, and the right kind of animal.

**Pets:** They are looking for a happy, safe home where someone will take good care of them.

## Product description

It is an app that helps people find their dream pet.

## Features

**Contact a shelter worker:** An animal shelter worker can help every animal get a place to live happily and safely.

**Find the right animal:** A person who loves animals can help their local shelter and the people around them find their dream pet.

**Animals near you:** A pet can get adopted.
