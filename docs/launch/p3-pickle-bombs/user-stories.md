# User stories for Find me

Team: Pickle bombs

## Contact a shelter worker

As an animal shelter worker, I want to help every animal get a place to live happily and safely, so that I can help people find the perfect pet and take in more pets that need a home.

## Find the right animal

As a person who loves animals, I want to help out my local animal shelter and the people around me looking for their dream pet, so that I can know that every animal out there alone will finally get a spot in a shelter and get adopted.

## Animals near you

As a pet, I want to get adopted by someone who will take good care of me, so that I can live with a human who loves me.
