## About

A woodland strawberry is a flower in the rose family. Naturally occurring across
the Northern Hemisphere, it is also known as a wild strawberry and a wood
strawberry. It has dark green leaves and bright red fruits. The fruits are small
yet flavorful.

## Care

1. Light. Full sun to part shade. It grows best in full sun but will put up with
   a shadier spot, which is why it does well at the edge of woods.
2. Soil. Fertile soil with plenty of compost in it, holding medium moisture and
   draining well.
3. Water. Keep it evenly moist. It does not want to sit in water.
4. Size. It stays small, about 4 to 8 inches tall, so it will not crowd taller
   plants.
5. Runners. It spreads by runners that root wherever they touch down. Pinch the
   runners off to keep one plant tidy in a container, or let them run to fill a
   bed. Some varieties are bred not to make runners at all.
6. Fruit. The berries are small and sweet, and a plant makes only about two or
   three a week. Pick them as they ripen, because unpicked fruit will drop seed
   and start new plants.
