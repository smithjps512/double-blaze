## About

- It handles extreme summer heat, drought and even road salt with ease once
  established.
- It won Perennial Plant of the Year in 1992 from the Perennial Plant
  Association.
- It is native to the southern United States, in states such as Florida and
  Texas.

## Care

1. Low maintenance.
2. Thrives best in full sun, 6 to 8 hours every day.
3. Poor, rocky or sandy soil.
4. 1 inch of water every week.
5. Little to no fertilizer needed.
6. Good drainage of soil.
7. Wet or soggy soil harms the plant. Water regularly until the roots take hold.
