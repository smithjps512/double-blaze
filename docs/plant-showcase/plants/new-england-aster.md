## About

- Native range: central and eastern North America
- Plant type: herbaceous perennial
- Mature size: 2 to 6 feet tall and 1 to 3 feet wide
- Bloom time: late summer through October, until the first hard frost
- Flower color: purple, pink, lavender or white with orange-yellow centers

### Growing requirements

- Sunlight: prefers full sun, though it tolerates light partial shade. It needs
  at least 6 to 8 hours of direct sunlight each day to grow and bloom its best.
- Soil: adapts to clay, loam or sandy soils, but prefers rich, moist, well
  draining conditions.
- Water: needs medium to moist soil. It tolerates short dry spells once
  established.

## Care

- Pinching: pinch the stems back by 1 to 2 inches in late spring, May to early
  June, to encourage a bushier, shorter plant that is less likely to flop over.
- Placement: lower leaves can turn brown and drop by flowering time, so plant
  them behind shorter perennials to hide the bare lower stems.
- Airflow: space plants 1 to 2 feet apart for good airflow, which prevents
  fungal leaf diseases.
- Dividing: divide large clumps every 2 to 3 years in the spring to keep the
  plant vigorous.
