# CO2 Dragster Project Reflection

**Course:** Intro to Technology
**Time:** at least 30 minutes (most students will need 35 to 45)
**Turned in:** online, through the reflection tool
**Graded on:** thoughtfulness, specific evidence, and honesty, not on whether your car won

## Why we reflect

Engineers do not stop when the product ships. They look back at what worked,
what did not, and what they would change, so the next build is better. This
is your chance to do that for the CO2 dragster, and to tell me what to keep
and what to change for next year's class.

## The rules for a strong answer

1. **Be specific.** Name a real moment, a measurement, a time, a tool. "It was
   fun" earns little credit. "Sanding the nose down to 1/4 inch took two
   class periods, and I learned to sand with the grain" earns full credit.
2. **Explain why.** Every opinion gets a reason.
3. **Be honest.** Telling me a part of the project was boring or frustrating
   will not hurt your grade. Vague answers will.
4. **Meet the word minimum.** Each written answer shows a minimum. Treat the
   minimum as the floor, not the goal.

## Part 1: Snapshot (about 5 minutes)

**1.1 Rate each phase.** For each one, give two ratings from 1 to 5: how much
you enjoyed it, and how well you think you did.

| Phase | Enjoyed (1 to 5) | How well I did (1 to 5) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Design and sketching | | |
| Building in the shop | | |
| Dragster Lab (virtual) | | |
| Race Day | | |

**1.2 Your numbers.** Your best race time in seconds (1.2a), and the time
you expected before the race (1.2b). If you do not know them, write "don't know".

**1.3 Three words** that describe your experience with this project.

## Part 2: Phase by phase (about 15 minutes)

### Design and sketching

**2.1** Describe one design decision you made on paper, such as the body
shape, the weight, how thin you cut it, or where the axles go. Why did you
choose it, and how did it work out once the car was real? *(60 words)*

**2.2** Look at your final car next to your original drawing. What changed
between the drawing and the car, and why? *(40 words)*

### Building in the shop

**2.3** Describe the hardest moment in the shop. What went wrong or got
difficult, what did you do about it, and what did it teach you about the
tools or the material? *(60 words)*

**2.4** Name one skill you got better at during the build, and describe
how you know you improved. *(30 words)*

### Dragster Lab (virtual)

**2.5** Compare your virtual car to your real car. Did the Lab predict a
time close to your real time? Give the numbers if you have them, and explain
why they matched or did not. *(50 words)*

**2.6** Did the Dragster Lab help you build a better real car? What would
make it more useful? *(40 words)*

### Race Day

**2.7** Explain your race result using at least two of these: mass, drag
(air resistance), friction, thrust, alignment. *(60 words)*

**2.8** We timed runs from 30 fps video, so each time is only good to about
0.05 seconds. What did that teach you about measuring things? Would you trust
a 0.02 second win? Why or why not? *(40 words)*

## Part 3: Keep, change, add, cut (about 10 minutes)

**3.1** What did you like most about the whole project, and why? *(50 words)*

**3.2** What did you dislike or find most frustrating, and what would have
made it better? *(50 words)*

**3.3** If you built a new CO2 car tomorrow, name three things you would do
differently. Explain each one. *(20 words each)*

**3.4 Add.** Name one thing you would add to this project for next year's
class, and explain how it would help them learn. *(40 words)*

**3.5 Cut or shorten.** Name one thing you would remove, shorten, or change
for next year, and explain why. *(40 words)*

## Part 4: Looking back (about 5 minutes)

**4.1** Write a short note to a student starting this project next year. What
do you wish someone had told you on day one? *(60 words)*

**4.2** Rate your own effort on this project from 1 to 10, and back up the
number with evidence. *(40 words)*

**4.3** Real engineers design, build, test, and redesign. Where in this
project did you go through that loop, even once? If you did not get to,
where would you have? *(50 words)*

**4.4** *(Optional)* Anything else you want me to know about your project?

---

**Total minimum:** 775 words across 21 required answers (3.3 counts as
three), plus the ratings. 4.4 is optional.

## The online tool

Students open https://doubleblaze.solutions/trail-crew/dragster/race-day/reflection.
No password is needed. Answers save on their device
as they type, every box shows a live word count, and **Turn in** stays locked
until every answer meets its minimum. Answers that pad the count by repeating
words are flagged. Read, score and comment at **Teacher > Project
reflections** (`/trail-crew/dragster/race-day/teacher/reflections`), where
you can also read every answer to one question side by side and download a
CSV.

## Teacher scoring guide (20 points)

| Points | What it looks like |
| --- | --- |
| 8 | **Specific evidence.** Real moments, numbers, tools, and measurements, not general statements. |
| 6 | **Reasoning.** Opinions and results come with a "because". Part 2.7 uses physics correctly. |
| 4 | **Forward thinking.** Changes, additions, and advice are concrete and doable. |
| 2 | **Complete.** Every required answer meets its minimum. |
