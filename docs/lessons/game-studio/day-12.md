# Day 12: Checks a builder could test

No em dashes anywhere in this document or any copy it generates.

At the end of today, every level has at least 2 checks, each a number or a clear yes or no.

## Again (5 minutes)

1. Read your three level stories out loud.
2. Point at which player type each one is for.

## Watch (5 to 10 minutes)

The teacher does this on the projector, start to finish. Nobody types until it
is done.

1. **Open** Demo Studio's level 1. Under the story, add a check: "The jump
   clears a 2 tile wall." Say: a check is something a builder can test with a
   yes or a no.
2. **Add** a bad one: "The level is fun." Ask: could the builder test that?
   (No.) Delete it.
3. **Add** another good one: "There are exactly 3 gaps, each 2 tiles wide."
   Say: numbers make checks testable. "Some gaps" is not.
4. **Show** the Definition of Done box under the story. Point at "at least 2
   checks" turning green.

Say out loud: if a check has no number and no yes or no, nobody can tell if it came true.

## Do (25 to 30 minutes)

1. *(again)* Open your studio. Read each level story out loud.
2. **(today)** Add 2 checks to every level. The Developer leads: could you
   build and test it?
3. **(today)** Put a number in every check you can: tiles, seconds, hearts,
   enemies, points.
4. **(today)** The QA Lead reads each check and says "yes" or "no" for how they
   would test it. Any check they cannot test gets rewritten.
5. **(today)** Look at your Mechanics tab. Does every mechanic used in a level
   have a check somewhere?

Stuck? Turn the rule from your Mechanics tab into a check: "Bolt jumps 3 tiles high" becomes "The jump clears a 3 tile wall but not a 4 tile wall."

## Done when

Every level shows at least 2 checks, and the QA Lead can say how to test each one.
