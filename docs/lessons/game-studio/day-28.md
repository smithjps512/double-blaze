# Day 28: A store page and a trailer pitch

No em dashes anywhere in this document or any copy it generates.

At the end of today, every studio has a store page written and a 30 second trailer pitch rehearsed for launch day.

## Again (5 minutes)

1. Say your game's one-liner from the Pitch tab, from memory.

## Watch (5 to 10 minutes)

The teacher does this on the projector, start to finish. Nobody types until it
is done.

1. **Show** a store page layout on the board: the game name, the one-liner,
   three bullet points of what you do, one line about the feeling, who it is
   for, and one "what players said" quote from a Love it report.
2. **Fill it in** for Demo Studio, using only things already in the design doc
   and on /play. Say: you already wrote all of this. Today you choose the
   best of it.
3. **Pitch** a 30 second trailer out loud: hook (the best moment), what you do
   (the core loop), how it feels, the call ("Play it on /play.").

Say out loud: your store page is the promise. Your game has to keep it.

## Do (25 to 30 minutes)

1. *(again)* Your tutorial level is green.
2. **(today)** Write the store page on paper or a slide: name, one-liner, three
   bullets, the feeling, who it is for, one real quote from a Love it report.
3. **(today)** Write the 30 second trailer pitch: hook, loop, feeling, call.
4. **(today)** Pick who says which part. Everyone in the studio speaks.
5. **(today)** Rehearse it twice, with a timer, in front of another studio.

Stuck? Your hook is the drawing on the back of your day 4 pitch card.

## Done when

Every studio has a store page and a trailer pitch under 30 seconds that everyone speaks in.
