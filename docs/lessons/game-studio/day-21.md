# Day 21: v1 is live: play your own, then playtest

No em dashes anywhere in this document or any copy it generates.

At the end of today, every studio has played its own v1, written what surprised it, and watched another studio's player play it silently.

## Again (5 minutes)

Roles rotate today: everybody takes the next role on their card.

1. Say your game's feeling from the Pitch tab. You are about to find out if v1
   gives it.

## Watch (5 to 10 minutes)

The teacher does this on the projector, start to finish. Nobody types until it
is done.

1. **Open** [/play](https://doubleblaze.solutions/play). Show the studio games
   at the top. Open Demo Studio's v1 and play it for a minute.
2. **Point** at one thing that is exactly what the doc said, and one thing that
   is thin. Open the Design doc tab and show why it is thin: the doc was thin
   there. Say: Double Blaze built only what you wrote.
3. **Show** the playtest protocol on the board:
   - The player plays and **thinks out loud**: "I am trying to ___."
   - The studio **watches silently**. No helping, no explaining, no pointing.
   - The QA Lead writes down every place the player got stuck or surprised.
4. **Say**: if you have to explain your game, the game did not explain itself.

Say out loud: watch silently. The moment you explain, you stop learning what the player really sees.

## Do (25 to 30 minutes)

1. **(today)** Your studio plays your own v1. 10 minutes. Everyone plays.
2. **(today)** The Producer writes: what is exactly like the doc, what is
   different, what is thin.
3. **(today)** Swap with another studio. One of their players plays your game,
   thinking out loud. Your studio watches silently. The QA Lead takes notes.
4. **(today)** Then you do the same for them.
5. **(today)** Back together: read the QA Lead's notes. Circle the place the
   player was most stuck.

Stuck? If the player goes quiet, the QA Lead may ask only one thing: "What are you trying to do?"

## Done when

Every studio has notes from its own play and from a silent playtest, with the stuck moment circled.

QA other studios' games, and send real QA reports.
