# Day 30: Retrospective and portfolio

No em dashes anywhere in this document or any copy it generates.

At the end of today, every studio has run a retrospective and every student has a one page portfolio of their part in the game.

## Again (5 minutes)

1. Read the best Love it your studio got yesterday.

## Watch (5 to 10 minutes)

The teacher does this on the projector, start to finish. Nobody types until it
is done.

1. **Draw** three columns on the board: **Went well**, **Change next time**,
   **What we learned about making things**. Say: real studios do this after
   every launch. It is called a retrospective.
2. **Fill in** one sticky note per column for the whole class's six weeks.
   For example: went well, "the bad days found real bugs"; change, "write the
   rules with numbers sooner"; learned, "the doc is the game".
3. **Show** the portfolio page: the game name, my roles, one thing I made
   (a level story, a mechanic, a QA report), one check I wrote that came true,
   and one sentence on what a product manager does.

Say out loud: the most useful thing you made in six weeks is not the game. It is knowing how to make the next one.

## Do (25 to 30 minutes)

1. **(today)** As a studio: three sticky notes each, one per column. Read them
   out loud and group them.
2. **(today)** The Producer picks one change the whole studio agrees on for
   next time.
3. **(today)** On your own: your one page portfolio. Game name, your roles,
   one thing you made, one check that came true, what a product manager does.
4. **(today)** Read your "what a product manager does" sentence to the person
   next to you.
5. **(today)** Hand in the portfolio.

Stuck? Look back at your studio's design doc and find something you wrote. That is your one thing you made.

## Done when

Every studio has a retrospective on the board and every student has handed in a one page portfolio.
