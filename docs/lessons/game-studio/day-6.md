# Day 6: Pitch and Players, in Game Studio

No em dashes anywhere in this document or any copy it generates.

At the end of today, every studio is signed in to Game Studio with its Pitch and at least two Players written.

## Again (5 minutes)

Roles rotate today: everybody takes the next role on their card.

1. The new Producer says the studio's pitch out loud from the index card.

## Watch (5 to 10 minutes)

The teacher does this on the projector, start to finish. Nobody types until it
is done.

1. **Open** [Game Studio](https://doubleblaze.solutions/studio). Pick period 3,
   type **Demo Studio**, open it. Say: everyone in your studio types the same
   studio name, so you all open the same doc. The Producer types; the others
   read along and press Refresh.
2. **Open** the **Pitch** tab. Fill in the game name, the one-liner ("It's a
   ___ game where you ___"), the feeling and the genre, copying from a pitch
   card. Say: the feeling box is the purpose. It is the most important box
   on this tab.
3. **Open** the **Players** tab. Add two player types and what each wants.
   Read the hint that appears when there is only one. Add the person who says
   yes as a third.
4. **Point** at the save message in the corner. Say: it saves by itself.

Say out loud: the design doc is the game. If it is not in the doc, Double Blaze cannot build it.

## Do (25 to 30 minutes)

1. *(again)* The Producer says the pitch out loud. The studio agrees on the
   exact one-liner.
2. **(today)** The Producer signs in: period 3, your studio name. Everyone else
   opens the same studio on their Chromebook to read along.
3. **(today)** Pitch tab: game name, one-liner, the feeling, genre.
4. **(today)** Players tab: at least two player types, each with one sentence
   of what they want. The Game Designer leads this.
5. **(today)** Add whoever says yes. The QA Lead asks: what would make a
   parent say no?
6. **(today)** Read the Pitch and Players out loud to the studio next to you.

Stuck? The hints under each box say what is missing. Read them out loud before asking.

## Done when

Every studio's Pitch tab is full and its Players tab has at least two player types with a sentence each.
