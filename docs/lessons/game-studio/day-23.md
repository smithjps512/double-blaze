# Day 23: Triage: bad days, and must, should, could

No em dashes anywhere in this document or any copy it generates.

At the end of today, every studio has turned its bugs into bad days and sorted everything it wants to change into must, should and could.

## Again (5 minutes)

1. Read one QA report you sent yesterday. Could the other studio follow it?

## Watch (5 to 10 minutes)

The teacher does this on the projector, start to finish. Nobody types until it
is done.

1. **Open** /play signed in as Demo Studio. Show **What testers found** with
   the approved reports.
2. **Press** **Turn into a bad day** on a bug. It opens Game Studio with the bug
   as a scenario. Pick the level it belongs to. Fill in the Given if it is
   empty.
3. **Draw** three columns on the board: **Must** (the game is broken or
   unfair without it), **Should** (much better with it), **Could** (nice
   someday). Sort three reports out loud.
4. **Say**: v2 gets your musts, then shoulds if there is room. Studios that
   try to fix everything fix nothing well.

Say out loud: triage is deciding what not to fix yet. That is the hardest and most important call a producer makes.

## Do (25 to 30 minutes)

1. *(again)* Open /play. Read every approved report about your game.
2. **(today)** For every bug: Turn into a bad day, and put it on the right
   level.
3. **(today)** Make the three columns on paper. Every report and every note
   from your own playtest goes in one column.
4. **(today)** The Producer gets the final say on what is a must. Everyone else
   argues for one change, once.
5. **(today)** Pick at most 3 musts and 2 shoulds for v2. Star them.

Stuck? Ask: would a new player quit because of this? If yes, it is a must.

## Done when

Every bug is a bad day in Game Studio, and the studio has at most 3 musts and 2 shoulds starred for v2.

Write the starred changes as level stories for v2.
