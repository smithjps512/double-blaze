# Day 5: Pitch day, and studios formed

No em dashes anywhere in this document or any copy it generates.

At the end of today, every student is in a studio of 3 or 4 with one game, one studio name, and a role for next week.

## Again (5 minutes)

1. Say your pitch to the person next to you once more, under 30 seconds.

## Watch (5 to 10 minutes)

The teacher does this on the projector, start to finish. Nobody types until it
is done.

1. **Explain** how today works: pitches, then everyone picks, then studios.
   Say: you are not voting for your friends, you are joining a game you want
   to make for six weeks.
2. **Pitch** the demo game yourself, from a card, in 30 seconds. Say: that is
   the length. The timer is on the board.
3. **Show** how picking works: every student gets three dots (stickers or
   marker dots) to put on the pitch cards posted on the wall. You may not
   dot your own card.
4. **Show** how studios form: the cards with the most dots become studios.
   Their pitchers stand by them. Everybody else joins one, 3 or 4 per
   studio. If a studio is full, go to your next dot.

Say out loud: the best idea in the room is not always yours. A studio that chose its game is a studio that will finish it.

## Do (25 to 30 minutes)

1. **(today)** Pitches, 30 seconds each, in a fast line. Post your card on the
   wall after you pitch. (about 12 minutes)
2. **(today)** Walk the wall and place your three dots. (3 minutes)
3. **(today)** Top cards become studios. Join one: 3 or 4 per studio.
   (5 minutes)
4. **(today)** In your studio: pick a studio name. Never a real name.
5. **(today)** Pick roles for week 2: Producer, Game Designer, Developer, QA
   Lead. Write them on the back of your role cards. Roles rotate every Monday.
6. **(today)** The Producer writes the studio name, the game name and the
   period on a sticky note for the teacher.

Stuck? Two studios want the same idea? Combine them only if the pitchers agree. Otherwise, the one with more dots keeps it and the other pitches its second idea.

## Done when

The teacher has a sticky note from every studio with a studio name, a game, and four roles.

## Tomorrow adds

The design doc starts. Your studio signs in to Game Studio and writes the Pitch and the Players.
