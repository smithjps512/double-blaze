# Day 7: Core loop, win and lose

No em dashes anywhere in this document or any copy it generates.

At the end of today, every studio's Core loop tab has 3 or 4 verbs, a way to win and a way to lose.

## Again (5 minutes)

1. Open your studio in Game Studio. The Producer reads the feeling from the
   Pitch tab out loud.
2. Name your player types without looking.

## Watch (5 to 10 minutes)

The teacher does this on the projector, start to finish. Nobody types until it
is done.

1. **Open** Demo Studio's **Core loop** tab. Type 3 verbs in order: run,
   collect, dodge. Say: these repeat. Read them as a loop: run, collect, dodge,
   run again.
2. **Type** how you win: "Reach the trophy at the end of level 3." Then how you
   lose: "Run out of 3 hearts." Say: a game with no way to lose has no
   tension, and tension is part of the feeling.
3. **Test** the loop against the feeling from yesterday. Ask: does run,
   collect, dodge make you feel brave? If not, which verb would?
4. **Mark** the core verb. Say: this is the one we protect.

Say out loud: the loop is what the player does a hundred times. Win and lose are why they care.

## Do (25 to 30 minutes)

1. *(again)* Open your studio. Check the Pitch and Players are still right.
2. **(today)** Core loop tab: 3 or 4 verbs in the order they repeat. The Game
   Designer leads.
3. **(today)** Mark your core verb.
4. **(today)** How you win. Something a player could see on the screen.
5. **(today)** How you lose. Something a player could see on the screen.
6. **(today)** The QA Lead asks: does this loop give the feeling on the Pitch
   tab? If not, change a verb.

Stuck? Say what the player does in the first ten seconds of the game. Those are usually your verbs.

## Done when

Every studio's Core loop tab has 3 or 4 verbs, a marked core verb, a win and a lose.
