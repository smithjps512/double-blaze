# Day 22: QA other studios' games

No em dashes anywhere in this document or any copy it generates.

At the end of today, every student has sent at least two QA reports on other studios' games, with exact steps.

## Again (5 minutes)

1. Say the three parts of a bug report from /play: what did you do, what
   happened, what did you expect.

## Watch (5 to 10 minutes)

The teacher does this on the projector, start to finish. Nobody types until it
is done.

1. **Open** [/play](https://doubleblaze.solutions/play) and a studio game. Press
   **QA it**. Choose **Bug**.
2. **Fill it in** out loud with a real bug from playing: the screen, the exact
   steps ("I jumped at the left wall twice"), what happened, what you
   expected.
3. **Show** a weak one: "it's broken". Ask: could the studio make it happen
   again? Say: steps are the gift. Without them, the bug cannot be fixed.
4. **Remind** them: Love it reports matter too, and they should say exactly
   what worked. "So cool" helps less than "the magnet pull made me feel
   chased."

Say out loud: a good bug report is one somebody else can follow to make the bug happen again.

## Do (25 to 30 minutes)

1. *(again)* Your studio's notes from yesterday's playtest are open.
2. **(today)** Play at least three other studios' games on /play.
3. **(today)** Send at least two QA reports each. At least one Bug or
   Confusing, with exact steps.
4. **(today)** Send at least one Love it, saying exactly what worked.
5. **(today)** The QA Lead checks your studio's reports before sending: could
   the other studio follow the steps?

Stuck? Try weird things on purpose: walk backwards, stand still, press every button, jump at walls.

## Done when

Every student has sent at least two QA reports, and at least one has steps another studio could follow.

## After class

Approve QA reports at [/play/teacher](https://doubleblaze.solutions/play/teacher)
before tomorrow. Studios only see approved reports, and tomorrow they triage them.
