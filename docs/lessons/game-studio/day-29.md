# Day 29: Launch day showcase

No em dashes anywhere in this document or any copy it generates.

At the end of today, every studio has launched: pitched its game, watched the class play it, and collected launch day QA reports.

## Again (5 minutes)

1. Rehearse your trailer pitch once, in your studio, with the timer.

## Watch (5 to 10 minutes)

The teacher does this on the projector, start to finish. Nobody types until it
is done.

1. **Explain** the showcase: each studio gives its trailer pitch and shows its
   store page. Then everyone plays.
2. **Show** the rule for playing today: every player sends one Love it and one
   Bug or Idea for every game they play, on /play.
3. **Start** the showcase with Demo Studio's 30 second trailer, so they see
   the length one more time.

Say out loud: you started six weeks ago with a word on a sticky note. Today it is a game other people are playing.

## Do (25 to 30 minutes)

1. **(today)** Trailer pitches, 30 seconds each, every studio, with the store
   page on the projector. (about 10 minutes)
2. **(today)** Open play on /play. Play at least four studios' games.
3. **(today)** For every game: one Love it and one Bug or Idea.
4. **(today)** Each studio keeps one Chromebook on its own game for visitors,
   with the QA Lead watching silently.
5. **(today)** Last 3 minutes: each Producer reads the best Love it they got.

Stuck? If nobody is playing your game, invite somebody. Say your one-liner.

## Done when

Every studio has pitched, and every student has sent a Love it and a Bug or Idea for at least four games.

## Before class

Invite a visitor if you can: another teacher, an administrator, a parent. A
real audience changes how studios pitch. Approve launch day reports at
[/play/teacher](https://doubleblaze.solutions/play/teacher) after class.
