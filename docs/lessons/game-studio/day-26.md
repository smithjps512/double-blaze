# Day 26: v2 is live: verify against your checks

No em dashes anywhere in this document or any copy it generates.

At the end of today, every studio has tested v2 against the checks for every change it asked for, and knows which came true.

## Again (5 minutes)

Roles rotate today: everybody takes the next role on their card.

1. Read your starred musts and shoulds from day 23 out loud.

## Watch (5 to 10 minutes)

The teacher does this on the projector, start to finish. Nobody types until it
is done.

1. **Open** [/play](https://doubleblaze.solutions/play) and Demo Studio's v2.
2. **Open** the design doc next to it. For one must, read its check and test it
   on the projector. Say "yes" or "no" out loud.
3. **Show** a check that passed and a bad day that now works. Then one that did
   not. Ask: is it the build or the doc this time?
4. **Say**: this is how a real studio finishes a version. Not "it looks good".
   Every check, yes or no.

Say out loud: a version is done when every check says yes, not when it looks done.

## Do (25 to 30 minutes)

1. **(today)** Play your v2 as a studio. 5 minutes, everyone.
2. **(today)** The QA Lead reads every check for every change you asked for.
   Test each one. Yes or no.
3. **(today)** Run every bad day you wrote from a bug. Does the game do what the
   Then says now?
4. **(today)** For each no, write a QA report on your own game, with steps.
5. **(today)** The Producer writes the score: how many checks passed out of how
   many?

Stuck? Test one check at a time. Read it, play only that, say yes or no.

## Done when

Every studio has a score for its checks and a QA report for every check that did not pass.
