# Day 13: Happy path scenarios

No em dashes anywhere in this document or any copy it generates.

At the end of today, every level has a happy path scenario in Given, When, Then.

## Again (5 minutes)

1. Pick one check from each level and say how you would test it.

## Watch (5 to 10 minutes)

The teacher does this on the projector, start to finish. Nobody types until it
is done.

1. **Remind** them of Given, When, Then from Launch Pad. Given is what is
   already true, When is what the player does, Then is what the game does
   back.
2. **Open** Demo Studio's level 1. Press **+ Happy path**. Type: Given Bolt is
   standing before the first gap, When the player presses A, Then Bolt jumps
   across and lands on the other side.
3. **Show** that the When is something the player does with the controls from
   the Mechanics tab. Say: if your When uses a button that is not on the
   Mechanics tab, one of them is wrong.
4. **Write** one more for level 2: Given the crane is in view, When the player
   walks behind it, Then a hidden battery appears.

Say out loud: a happy path is the level going exactly right. It is the clearest way to show a builder what you mean.

## Do (25 to 30 minutes)

1. *(again)* Open your studio. Check every level has 2 checks.
2. **(today)** Add a happy path to every level: Given, When, Then.
3. **(today)** The Developer checks each When against the Mechanics tab
   controls.
4. **(today)** The Game Designer checks each Then: is this the moment that
   gives the feeling on the Pitch tab?
5. **(today)** Read one happy path to another studio. They tell you what they
   picture. Fix any word they got wrong.

Stuck? Given: where is the hero? When: which button? Then: what does the player see?

## Done when

Every level has a complete happy path, and each When uses a control from the Mechanics tab.
