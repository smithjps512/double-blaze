# Day 27: Onboarding: a tutorial level story

No em dashes anywhere in this document or any copy it generates.

At the end of today, every studio has written a tutorial level story that teaches a brand new player the core verb without words.

## Again (5 minutes)

1. Say your check score from yesterday, and one check that did not pass.

## Watch (5 to 10 minutes)

The teacher does this on the projector, start to finish. Nobody types until it
is done.

1. **Ask** the class: in day 21's playtests, where did new players get stuck
   first? Most studios will say: right at the start.
2. **Say**: great games teach by doing. The first level is the tutorial, and
   the best tutorials use almost no words.
3. **Write** a tutorial level story for Demo Studio: "As a new player, I want
   to learn to jump by needing to jump right away, so I can start playing
   without reading anything." Checks: "The first gap appears within 3 seconds.
   It is 1 tile wide, so you cannot fail it." Happy path and bad day.
4. **Show** the bad day: the player stands still. Then: after 5 seconds, a
   small arrow points at the gap.

Say out loud: if your first level needs instructions, your first level is not finished.

## Do (25 to 30 minutes)

1. *(again)* Any check that failed yesterday has a QA report.
2. **(today)** Write a tutorial level story for a brand new player.
3. **(today)** Two checks: how fast does the player do the core verb, and how
   can they not fail it the first time?
4. **(today)** A happy path, and a bad day for the player who does nothing.
5. **(today)** Read it to another studio's player who has never played your
   game. Can they tell you what they will learn?

Stuck? The tutorial teaches one thing: your core verb. Everything else waits for level 2.

## Done when

Every studio has a tutorial level story that is green on the Definition of Done.
