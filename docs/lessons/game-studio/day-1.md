# Day 1: What a game studio is

No em dashes anywhere in this document or any copy it generates.

At the end of today, every student can name the four studio roles and has taken one game apart into its core loop and its feeling.

## Again (5 minutes)

There is no yesterday. Instead, one minute of quiet: on a sticky note, write
the last game you played and one word for how it made you feel. Stick it on
the board on the way in.

## Watch (5 to 10 minutes)

The teacher does this on the projector, start to finish. Nobody types until it
is done.

1. **Read** three sticky notes from the board out loud. Point out that
   nobody wrote "it had good graphics". They wrote feelings: tense, proud,
   funny, calm. Say: that word is what the game is for.
2. **Show** the roles slide or write the four roles on the board: **Producer**,
   **Game Designer**, **Developer**, **QA Lead**. One sentence each, from the
   README's role table. Hand out the role cards.
3. **Play** one short game on the projector for two minutes. W.C.O 27 on
   [/play](https://doubleblaze.solutions/play) works well. Narrate what you
   keep doing: "I jump, I grab the ball, I dodge, I jump again."
4. **Write** on the board what you just said, as a circle of verbs with arrows:
   jump, collect, dodge, back to jump. Say: this circle is the **core loop**.
   It is what the player does over and over.
5. **Ask** the class: what did it feel like? Write the feeling in the middle
   of the circle.

Say out loud: a game is a few verbs the player does over and over, and a feeling. Studios decide both on purpose, before anybody builds anything.

## Do (25 to 30 minutes)

Pairs today. Studios form on Friday.

1. **(today)** Play a game for five minutes. Use one from the board list. One
   plays, one watches and writes down every verb the player does.
2. **(today)** Swap and play for five minutes more. The new watcher adds verbs.
3. **(today)** Circle the 3 or 4 verbs that happened the most. Draw them as a
   loop with arrows, the way the teacher did.
4. **(today)** In the middle of the loop, write the feeling in one word.
5. **(today)** Write which role you think you would be best at, and which one
   you have never tried. Keep the card.

Stuck? Say out loud everything the player does, as "I ___". Every "I ___" is a verb.

## Done when

Every pair has one loop of 3 or 4 verbs with a feeling in the middle, and can say it out loud.

## Tomorrow adds

Core loops in games everybody knows, and finding the one verb a game cannot live without.
