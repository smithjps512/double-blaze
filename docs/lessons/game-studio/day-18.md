# Day 18: Tilemaps and the camera

No em dashes anywhere in this document or any copy it generates.

At the end of today, every student has a level drawn as a tilemap, with walls, bigger than the screen, and a camera that follows the hero.

## Again (5 minutes)

1. Open your project. Collect a coin and lose a life to show both overlaps
   still work.

## Watch (5 to 10 minutes)

The teacher does this on the projector, start to finish. Nobody types until it
is done.

1. **Open** the **Scene** drawer. Drag **set tilemap to** into on start. Click
   the tilemap square. Make it bigger than the screen (32 by 16).
2. **Draw** the floor with tiles, then switch to the **wall** tool and draw
   walls over them. Say: walls are a rule. The hero cannot walk through them.
3. **Add** **camera follow sprite mySprite** from the Scene drawer. Walk to
   the edge. The camera follows.
4. **Open** Demo Studio's level 1 in Game Studio on the other half of the
   projector. Point at a check like "3 gaps, each 2 tiles wide". Say: a
   tilemap is where that check becomes real.

Say out loud: a tilemap is a level. Your Levels tab is the plan for it.

## Do (25 to 30 minutes)

1. *(again)* Score and lives work.
2. **(today)** A tilemap bigger than the screen, with a floor and walls.
3. **(today)** Camera follows the hero.
4. **(today)** Place your coins and enemies inside the level, not just on the
   starting screen.
5. **(today)** Open your studio's level 1 in Game Studio. Build one of its
   checks into the tilemap (a gap, a wall, a number of coins).

Stuck? If the hero walks through walls, you drew the tiles but not the walls. Use the wall tool in the tilemap editor.

## Done when

Every student's level is bigger than the screen, the camera follows, and one check from level 1 is built into it.
