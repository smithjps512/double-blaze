# Day 20: Test it against your own checks

No em dashes anywhere in this document or any copy it generates.

At the end of today, every studio has tested its mechanic against its checks and fixed the design doc where the build showed it was wrong or thin.

## Again (5 minutes)

1. Play your mechanic. Read yesterday's sticky notes of questions out loud.

## Watch (5 to 10 minutes)

The teacher does this on the projector, start to finish. Nobody types until it
is done.

1. **Open** Demo Studio's built jump next to its checks in Game Studio.
2. **Test** each check out loud, as yes or no: "The jump clears a 2 tile
   wall." Yes. "The jump does not clear a 4 tile wall." Yes.
3. **Show** a check that failed or could not be tested. Ask: is the build
   wrong, or the check? Say: sometimes the code is wrong. Sometimes the doc is.
4. **Fix** the doc on the projector: answer yesterday's "can Bolt jump in mid
   air?" by adding it to the rule, and add a bad day for it.

Say out loud: spec versus build: when they disagree, decide which one is right, then fix the other.

## Do (25 to 30 minutes)

1. *(again)* Your studio's mechanic still works.
2. **(today)** The QA Lead tests every check that uses this mechanic. Yes or
   no, out loud.
3. **(today)** For each no: is it the build or the doc? Fix whichever is wrong.
4. **(today)** Turn every sticky note question from yesterday into a rule
   change on the Mechanics tab, a check, or a bad day.
5. **(today)** The Producer writes one sentence in the studio's notes: what
   did building it teach us about writing it?

Stuck? If you cannot tell whether a check passed, the check is the problem. Give it a number.

## Done when

Every check for the mechanic has a yes or no, and every question from yesterday is now in the design doc.
