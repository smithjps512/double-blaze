# Day 16: MakeCode Arcade: sprites and moving

No em dashes anywhere in this document or any copy it generates.

At the end of today, every student has a hero sprite they drew themselves moving around the screen with the controller.

## Again (5 minutes)

Roles rotate today: everybody takes the next role on their card. This week
everybody codes, and the Developer is the studio's go-to person.

1. Say your game's core verb and the controls for it from your Mechanics tab.

## Watch (5 to 10 minutes)

The teacher does this on the projector, start to finish. Nobody types until it
is done.

1. **Open** [arcade.makecode.com](https://arcade.makecode.com) and press
   **New Project**. Name it after your studio. Point at the three parts: the
   block drawers on the left, the workspace, the game simulator.
2. **Open** the **Sprites** drawer. Drag **set mySprite to sprite of kind
   Player** into **on start**. Click the gray square and draw the hero in the
   image editor. Say: this is your hero from the World tab.
3. **Open** the **Controller** drawer. Drag **move mySprite with buttons**
   under it. Press the arrow keys on the simulator. The hero moves.
4. **Change** the speed numbers on that block (vx and vy, under the plus).
   Say: that number is a check. "The hero crosses the screen in 2 seconds" is
   something you can test.

Say out loud: every block is a rule. You are now the computer from the paper prototype.

## Do (25 to 30 minutes)

Everybody on their own Chromebook. Studios sit together and help each other.

1. **(today)** New project in MakeCode Arcade, named after your studio.
2. **(today)** A Player sprite. Draw your studio's hero.
3. **(today)** Move it with buttons. Try three speeds. Write down the one that
   feels right for your game's feeling.
4. **(today)** Change the background color in the **Scene** drawer to match
   your World tab's setting.
5. **(today)** Show your studio. The QA Lead tries to make the hero go off the
   screen. Can they? (Look for **stay in screen** in the Sprites drawer.)

Stuck? Every drawer is a color. Moving is in Controller (red), the hero is in Sprites (blue), the background is in Scene (green).

## Done when

Every student's hero moves with the arrow keys at a speed they chose and wrote down.
