# Day 11: Level stories

No em dashes anywhere in this document or any copy it generates.

At the end of today, every studio has at least 3 levels written as player stories in Game Studio.

## Again (5 minutes)

Roles rotate today: everybody takes the next role on their card.

1. The new Producer reads the three things you learned from the paper prototype.

## Watch (5 to 10 minutes)

The teacher does this on the projector, start to finish. Nobody types until it
is done.

1. **Open** Demo Studio's **Levels** tab. Say: each level is a player story,
   the same as on Launch Pad. As a, I want to, so I can.
2. **Write** level 1: "As a new player, I want to learn to jump over small
   gaps, so I can feel ready for the junkyard." Say: level 1 is often a
   lesson in disguise.
3. **Write** level 2 for a different player type: "As a collector, I want to
   find a hidden battery behind the crane, so I can finish with full power."
4. **Write** level 3 for a competitor: "As a competitor, I want a timed
   magnet maze, so I can beat my best time."
5. **Point** at how each level is for a different player from the Players
   tab. Say: three levels, three kinds of players.

Say out loud: a level is a story about what one kind of player wants to do. Make each level matter to somebody.

## Do (25 to 30 minutes)

1. *(again)* Open your studio. Read the Players tab out loud. Who are your
   player types?
2. **(today)** Levels tab: level 1 as a story for a new player. What do they
   learn?
3. **(today)** Level 2 and level 3, each for a different player type from your
   Players tab.
4. **(today)** Give every level a name, two or three words.
5. **(today)** The QA Lead reads every "so I can" out loud. If it repeats the
   "I want to", rewrite it.

Stuck? Use your core verb in level 1, a new mechanic in level 2, and both together in level 3.

## Done when

Every studio has at least 3 levels, each a complete story for a player type on its Players tab.
