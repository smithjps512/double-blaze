# Day 2: The review, and checking every line of it

No em dashes anywhere in this document or any copy it generates.

At the end of today, every team has a Design review in their chain of pages,
and has checked every finding on it against their own Figma file.

## Again (5 minutes)

From memory, faster than yesterday:

1. Open your design brief. Find the share box.
2. Paste your Figma link and a fresh one-line note. Share it. (This is also
   T10 in the making: count how many shares your team has sent.)

## Watch (5 to 10 minutes)

1. **Open** Claude Code with the Figma connector on. Type
   `/design-review <your demo team folder>` and press Enter. Narrate what it
   does as it goes: it reads the link from the database, lists the file's
   pages, reads each page, runs the checker, writes the page. Say: nothing
   here is a model deciding whether the design is good. It is a list of
   names on one side compared with a list of names on the other.
2. **Read** the findings it prints. Pick the first one. Open your demo Figma
   file beside it and show the thing it names. Say: that is what a finding
   is. Two documents disagreeing, and you can go and look.
3. **Show** the one wrong thing you left in the demo file being caught.
4. **Merge** the review's pull request and say the page arrives in the chain
   in about three minutes. Say: while it deploys, we run the next thing.
   That gap is normal. Real software has a gap between "done" and "live".
5. When it lands, **open** the demo team's design brief, point at **Design
   review** in the chain, open it, and show the same findings.

Say out loud: every line on this page is either true, or a bug in the review.
Your job today is to find out which.

## Do (25 to 30 minutes)

1. *(again)* Share your file again from the design brief with a fresh note.
   Run T10: keep sharing until the fifth one in ten minutes is refused, and
   write down what it said.
2. **(today)** Your teacher runs the review for your team. Watch the findings
   it prints and write down the first one. That is T11.
3. **(today)** While it deploys, open
   [Handing over your design](/build/handover.html) and go down the checklist
   against your file. Predict which findings the review will have. Write the
   predictions down.
4. **(today)** When the page is live, run T12: the **Design review** link is
   in your chain and the page carries your team name, the date, and the same
   findings your teacher saw.
5. **(today)** Run T13, the important one. Open the review beside your Figma
   file. For every finding, go and look. Is the card really without a page?
   Is the layer really missing? Is that number really not in your documents?
   Every finding is **pass** if it is true, **fail** if it is not. Write the
   exact finding down for every fail.
6. **(today)** Run T14: read "What is already right" and check that every
   line is something you actually did.
7. Compare with your predictions from step 3. What did you predict that the
   review did not find? What did it find that you did not predict?

Stuck? The helper on your design brief page has your review open now. Ask it
what a finding means.

## Done when

The recorder can read out T10 to T14, and every finding on your review has a
pass or a fail next to it.
