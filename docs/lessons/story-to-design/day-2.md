# Day 2: Approve it, and follow it through

No em dashes anywhere in this document or any copy it generates.

At the end of today, every team's story is in their file, its build card has
been rewritten from it, the card is on the board, and the team has compared
what the card says with what they predicted it would say.

## Again (5 minutes)

From memory, faster than yesterday:

1. Open **Write a story** from your board. Fill in the name, as a, I want, so
   that, for a second story. Do not send it. Close the tab.

## Watch (5 to 10 minutes)

1. **Open** yesterday's email on the projector. Click **Read it and decide**.
   Say: no sign-in. The link is the key, it names this one story, and it
   expires. Opening it changed nothing.
2. **Show** the page: the story on one side, Bus Buddy's current stories file
   on the other. Read the story once more. Press **Approve**.
3. Say what is now happening, in order, while it happens: the story is written
   into Bus Buddy's file. The build card for it is rewritten from the story,
   by rule, no model. The site rebuilds, about three minutes. And after that,
   Spark drafts the architecture change this story needs and puts it in my
   queue. That draft is the only thing in the whole chain a model writes, and
   it does not land until I read it and approve it.
4. **While it rebuilds**, open Bus Buddy's build cards page as it is now, and
   say what you expect the new card's **Done when** list to be. Write it on
   the board. Say: the card is the story with a finish line. If I know the
   story, I know the card.
5. **When it lands**, open Bus Buddy's stories page and find the story. Open
   the test plan and find its scenario. Open the build cards page and find the
   card. Compare its Done when with the board. Open the project board and show
   the card's row with a tick box per line.
6. **Open** the second email, the architecture draft. Click through, read the
   draft next to the current page, change one word, approve. Say: tomorrow
   Bus Buddy has a design brief, made from this, and that is what we design
   from.

Say out loud: nothing on those pages was typed by anybody. It all came from
the story. Change the story and all of it changes again.

## Do (25 to 30 minutes)

The teacher approves each team's Day 1 story during this time, from the
email, one after another. Teams do not wait for their turn to start.

1. *(again)* Your second story's first three boxes, from memory, not sent.
2. **(today)** Before your story is approved: open your build cards page and
   write down what you predict the card's Done when list will say. One line
   per criterion in your story, in your own words.
3. **(today)** When your teacher says yours is approved, wait about three
   minutes, then open your stories page. Your story is in it. Open the test
   plan page. Your scenario is in it.
4. **(today)** Open the build cards page. Find your card. Compare its Done when
   with your prediction. Which lines match? Which did you not predict? A
   criterion you wrote that did not become a Done when line is a criterion
   that could not be checked by looking. Fix it in the story, not the card.
5. **(today)** Open the project board. Your card is a row. Set it to
   **Building** if that is true. Tick nothing yet.
6. **(today)** Open your **architecture** page and read the note at the top
   about whether it has caught up. Say to each other which screen your story
   is going to need. Tomorrow that screen is the one you design.

Stuck? The **What next** page for your team says what the chain is waiting
on. It is usually right.

## Done when

Your story is in your file, your card is on the board, and your prediction
is next to the real Done when with the differences circled.

## Tomorrow adds

The screen. Starting it with AI, from the card's own words, and then reading
back what the AI made against the Done when list.

## For the teacher, after class

Approve or edit each team's architecture draft from the queue email before
tomorrow. Read each one beside the team's current page. The draft is right
about the boring parts; check the decision it made about the screen. A team
whose draft is not approved by Day 3 designs from their card instead of their
brief, which works, and they name their layers on Day 4 once the brief lands.
