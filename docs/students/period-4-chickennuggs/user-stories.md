# TrailRider user stories

Team: Chicken.nugg

## Shop

As a seller, I want to sell my bike, so that I can make money on a bike I do not
want.

  - You need a bike or equipment to sell
  - You need proof, a photo or a receipt
  - You need to be logged in
  Given I put my bike on a listing
  When I receive a message and coordinate with the buyer
  Then I meet up with the buyer, I receive cash and give the item

As a consumer, I want to buy a bike or bike equipment, so that I can acquire
bike equipment from the shop on TrailRiders.

  - Click on a listing on the store page
  - Click on what you want to buy and buy it
  Given I want to buy an item I like
  When I go to the location at the agreed time
  Then I pick up my purchase

## Trail map

As a consumer, I want to be able to look up trails and paths for mountain biking
near me, so that I can ride my bike through suitable trails for me.

  - Allow the app to track your location
  Given I want to see trails around me
  When I open the map feature
  Then I will be able to see the mountain biking trails near me

  Given I want to see the difficulty of a trail
  When I click on a trail
  Then I will be able to see the difficulty, reviews, and what the trail improves (core, legs, etc.)

  Given I want to see how long the trail is
  When I enter the map
  Then the length of all the trails will appear over the name of the trail

## Health and training AI

As a consumer, I want to give health and training advice, so that I can get
healthier and stronger and better at biking.

  Given I am in the chat box
  When I type a question about a training plan for me
  Then the AI responds with a correct educated answer

  Given I have asked 5 questions
  When I am talking to the AI
  Then the AI will stop answering you

  Given I am in the chat box
  When I reach my maximum questions
  Then I can ask more questions in 48 hours

  Given I am in the chat box
  When it has been 48 hours
  Then I can ask 5 more questions and the process starts over

## Signing in

As a person who is trying to log in or sign up for a TrailRider app, I want to
sign in, so that I can have a person interested in mountain biking log in or
sign up.

  Given I am on the login screen
  When I submit my email and username
  Then I am approved and able to explore the app
