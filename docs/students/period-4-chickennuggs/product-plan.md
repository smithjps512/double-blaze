# TrailRider

Team: Chicken.nugg

## Purpose

To service mountain biking communities to help them find trails and materials
easier.

## Who are the users

Mountain bikers, road bikers, hikers, sellers, and music creators.

**Mountain bikers:** The main users. They want to find good trails, get better
at riding, and buy and sell gear without the hassle.

**Road bikers and hikers:** They use the same trail map for normal trails, not
just mountain biking trails.

**Sellers:** People selling bikes, helmets, parts, and gear.

**Music creators:** People whose music gets suggested for trails.

## Product description

TrailRider is an app with a mountain bike shop for supplies, a map with a bunch
of trails, music suggestions, and health and training advice. The shop is
similar to Facebook Marketplace, where people post listings for bikes, helmets,
parts and so on. The map is full of trails with descriptions of the trails made
by bikers, along with a difficulty level. There is an event page where bikers
can find rides and events.

The shop allows bikers to avoid the hassle of searching on Google. The practice
advice makes bikers practice better. The map lets bikers choose good trails, and
the event page allows mountain bikers to find people like them and participate
in fun races.

## Features

**Shop:** A shop similar to Facebook Marketplace where riders can sell biking
related stuff like bike gloves, and people can buy them by messaging the sellers
on the built in messaging system.

**Trail map:** A map with a bunch of trails, reviews, a summary of the trail's
difficulty and what it improves (core, legs), and how long the trail is.

**Health and training AI:** An AI chatbot that gives health and riding advice
and practice advice for trails in your area.

**Music suggestions:** A playlist that shows suggested songs for whatever trail
you are on.

**Events page:** A page where people post different events for different places,
so bikers can find rides and races.

**Messaging:** A built in messaging system, so a buyer can message a seller
about a listing.
