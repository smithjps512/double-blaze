# Where this team's documents came from

Transcribed from Google Drive, folder `2nd period / room arrange`.

- `Create Your Product Planroomorganizer.pdf` and
  `Create Your Product room creator.pdf`: two plans for the same product. The
  plan here is the union of both.
- `User Story Exampleroom scan.pdf`: the scan system.
- `User Story Examplenyktil.pdf`: AI recognition.
- `User Story Exampleroomscan.pdf`: sign in and log in.

**Removed in transcription:** four students' names, three of them full names,
which appeared in a "shared with" line and a team roster line. This repository
is public.

Also changed: spelling corrected, template scaffolding removed. Nothing was
added.

Note for the team: two stories out of three describe building the AI rather than
using the product. "Create the AI, teach it to recognize objects, test and
improve it a countless number of times" is a project plan, not acceptance
criteria. Acceptance criteria say how you would know it works from the outside.
