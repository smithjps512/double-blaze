## Card 1: Scan system

**Your story.** As a creator user, I want to be able to scan my room, so that I can be ready to move the furniture.

**Done when:**
- [ ] Be able to scan and show the furniture clearly
- [ ] All furniture must look as it does in the actual room
- [ ] Must be able to successfully add and subtract

**Build it:** Architecture. Ask your teacher which feature number, then put it here.

**Patterns this probably needs:** **3** (Put something on the screen), **7** (Save something to the database). A first guess from the words in your own criteria, not a verdict. The order goes on your architecture page.
