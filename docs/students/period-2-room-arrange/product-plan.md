# AI Room Arranger

Team: Nyktilathraiophagos Productions

## Purpose

To rearrange your room without having to actually move the furniture. The only
work you have to do is scan the room.

## Who are the users

**People who like to rearrange things:** Anyone who enjoys moving furniture
around to see how it looks.

**People who are into decorating:** People who want to try a look before
committing to it.

**People who have recently moved:** People who want to work out where everything
goes in a new place.

## Product description

You scan your room using the app, then press a button to upload it. The app
creates a 3D visual of the room, the furniture becomes movable, and you can
rearrange the room however you want without actually moving anything. It can do
things that are very hard to undo in real life, for example changing the
backsplash in your kitchen.

## Features

**Scan system:** Insert a photo or video of the room and have the app read it.

**AI recognition:** An AI recognizes the objects in the room so each one can be
selected and moved.

**3D view:** An interactive 3D view of the room that you can rearrange.

**Sign in:** Make an account so you can save all the rooms you have captured.
