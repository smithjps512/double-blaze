# AI Room Arranger user stories

Team: Nyktilathraiophagos Productions

Revised: 2026-09-17

## Scan system

As a creator user, I want to be able to scan my room, so that I can be ready to
move the furniture.

  - Be able to scan and show the furniture clearly
  - All furniture must look as it does in the actual room
  - Must be able to successfully add and subtract
  Given to rearrange my room I need to scan my room
  When I scanned it and the scan got put into the AI
  Then the AI made an interactive 3D view of my room that I can use to rearrange it

## AI recognition

As a user, I want to make it so an AI can recognize objects, so that I can select
each object and move it anywhere around the room.

  - Create the AI
  - Teach it to recognize objects
  - Test and improve it a countless number of times
  Given I just moved into a new house
  When I tried to get all of my stuff from my old house organized to my liking
  Then I used the AI room designer and found how I wanted my house to look and where I wanted my stuff to go

## Sign in

As a consumer user, I want to be able to make an account, so that I can save all
the rooms I have pictured.

  - Must put in email or phone number
  - Username must be unique
  - Passwords must be eight characters long and have uppercase and lowercase letters
  Given I need to access a room I already recorded
  When I logged into my account
  Then I was able to access the room and rearrange things again

## People who like to rearrange things

As a user who likes to rearrange things, I want to be able to rearrange my room
in different ways and see how the ways look, so that I can see different ways I
will be able to rearrange my room.

  - Be able to show the furniture clearly
  - The user must be able to move the items that are in their room around
  - Must be able to successfully let the user move their view of their room so they can see different angles of their room

  Given to rearrange my room I need to scan my room
  When after I scanned it the scan got put into AI to make a 3D view of my room
  Then the AI made an interactive 3D view of my room that I can now use to rearrange my room to how I want it to look

## People who have recently moved

As a user who has recently moved, I want to be able to rearrange my room to see
where I want my things to go, so that I can move the items that I want in and
out of my room and see if that is the way I want it to look.

  - Be able to let the user move their items in and out of their room
  - The user must be able to move the items that are in their room around
  - Must be able to successfully let the user move their view of their room so they can see different angles of their room

  Given to rearrange my room I need to scan my room
  When after I scanned it the scan got put into AI to make a 3D view of my room
  Then the AI made an interactive 3D view of my room that I can now use to rearrange my room to how I want it to look

## People who are into decorating

As a user who is into decorating, I want to be able to decorate my room the way
that I want, so that I see different ways to decorate my room and whether I like
the way it is decorated or not.

  - Be able to move the items in their room
  - The user must be able to see different angles of their room and see if they like it or not
  - Must be able to let the user put in and take out the items in their room

  Given to rearrange my room I need to scan my room
  When after I scanned it the scan got put into AI to make a 3D view of my room
  Then the AI made an interactive 3D view of my room that I can now use to rearrange and move the items in my room to how I want them to look

## AI room Arranger

As a person who has a scanned room, I want to re-arrange objects in the room, so that I can see it before putting effort into decorating.

  - scanned room is clear
  - I can navigate room using wsad
  - objects are interactive

  Given I am in a scanned room
  When I select an object
  Then I can move it

  Given I am in a scanned room
  When I see an object I want to move
  Then I can navigate to it AND grab the object
