# Drone Dropper

Team: The McTriples

## Purpose

There are places that people live that are in rural areas and cannot get
delivery, but the Drone Dropper food app will deliver anywhere in Virginia
because we use a drone which will travel in the air, so no traffic waits.

## Who are the users

**People who do not have the time to cook:** They want food delivered fast.

**People in rural areas:** They cannot come to a fast food place because it is
not near them, and normal delivery apps will not go there.

**Drone loaders:** The people who pack the order into the drone and choose the
heated or chilled box.

## Product description

In the app we use drones to deliver the package so we would not wait in traffic.
If the delivery does not come in 2 hours the whole food is free. The app is free,
but there is a premium subscription if you want it. People who have the premium
subscription will have a heater in the bag that delivers, so it will not be cold,
and you can choose if you want it cold or hot. If you are allergic, there is a
button where it asks if you have any allergies, and it suggests every fast food
place you have eaten at for the past month.

## Features

**AI drones:** Drones with AI driving them that can deliver food as fast as
possible.

**Delivers anywhere in Virginia:** Delivers everywhere in Virginia, including
places other apps might not go.

**Temperature controlled boxes:** In the drone is a heater and a cooler for the
food, so you can choose hot or cold.

**Allergy notification:** Asks a question about whether you are allergic, and if
you are there is a tab that says what kind of allergy you have to fill in.

**Delivery time guarantee:** If the delivery does not come in 2 hours, the food
is free.
