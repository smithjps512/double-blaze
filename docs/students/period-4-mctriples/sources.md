# Where this team's documents came from

Transcribed from Google Drive, folder `period 4 / mctriples`.

- `Product Plan.pdf` and `Product Plan2.pdf`: two product plans for the same
  product, one per student. The plan here is the union of both, using their
  sentences.
- `My User Stories.pdf`, `My User Stories3.pdf`, `My User Stories4.pdf`: three
  user stories. Delivery, temperature controlled boxes, AI drones.
- `My User Stories5.pdf` in this folder is **not this team's work**. It is a
  Cuisinely story and was transcribed into `period-4-cuisinely` instead.

Two things for the team to settle, left as they wrote them rather than decided
here:

1. **The two plans disagree on the guarantee.** One says the food is free if
   delivery takes more than 2 hours, the other says 1 hour. The 2 hour version
   is used above because it came from the fuller plan. The team should pick.
2. **Two of the three stories are the same story.** The delivery story and the
   AI drones story have the same narrative and nearly the same acceptance
   criteria. That leaves the Allergy notification and the guarantee with no
   story at all.
