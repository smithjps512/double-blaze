# User stories for your product name

Team: your team name

A user story is one sentence in three parts. Who wants it, what they want, and
why it matters to them.

```
As a <user type>, I want <what they want>, so that <why it matters>.
```

The "so that" is the part people skip and it is the part that matters most. It
is the only thing in the sentence that tells you whether the feature is worth
building. If you cannot finish the "so that", you may not have a feature yet.

Under each story, write the acceptance criteria: how you would know it works.
Use Given, When, Then.

```
Given <what is already true>
When <what the user does>
Then <what the product does back>
```

The "When" line becomes a button or a box you can type in, and the "Then" line
is what happens when you press it. That is how your writing turns into
something clickable.

Put your stories under a heading naming the feature they belong to, so they end
up on the right screen.
