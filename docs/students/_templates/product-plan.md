## Purpose

Why should this product exist? One or two sentences. Not what it does, why it
matters. A good purpose names a person who has a problem and says what changes
for them.

Weak: "It is an app for sports."
Strong: "Middle school athletes miss practices because schedules change and the
news never reaches them in time."

## Who are the users

List every kind of person who will use this. Give each one a name and a
sentence about what they care about. Most products have more than one, and the
ones people forget are the adults: parents, teachers, coaches, whoever runs it.

## Product description

Describe what the product is to somebody who has never heard of it. Two to four
sentences. If you had thirty seconds in an elevator, this is what you would say.
