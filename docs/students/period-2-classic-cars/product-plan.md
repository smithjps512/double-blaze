# Product Plan

Team: Classic Cars

Draft: true

## Purpose

Lots of people think cars are cool but have no idea how they actually work. We
want a place where you can look at cool cars, learn what each part does, test
yourself on it, and mess around with what makes a car faster.

## Who are the users

**Kids who like cars:** They like the looks, the sounds and the speed, but
nobody has ever explained what is happening under the hood. They want it in
plain words, not a manual.

**People who want to test themselves:** They already know a bit and want to find
out how much.

## Product description

A site where you can browse cool cars and their stats, read a short page about
each part of a car and what it does, take a quiz on what you learned, and build
a car by choosing upgrades to see how much horsepower you end up with.

## Features

**Car gallery:** A list of cool cars. Click one to see its stats: year, top
speed, horsepower and what makes it special.

**Parts library:** A short page for each part of a car (engine, turbo, exhaust,
brakes, tires) explaining what it does, in plain words.

**Quiz:** Questions about what the parts do, with a score at the end.

**Horsepower builder:** Start with a stock engine and pick upgrades. The
horsepower number goes up as you add them.
