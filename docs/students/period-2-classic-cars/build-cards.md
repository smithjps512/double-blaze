# Classic Cars: build cards

Team: Classic Cars.

One card per story. Next stop: `build-architecture.md`. After that:
`docs/build/anvil-patterns.md`.

**Read this first.** Three of these four cards were drafted with your teacher
from what you said you liked about cars, so the first job is to read them and
change what is wrong. They are a starting point, not a verdict. The quiz card is
the story you wrote yourselves.

---

## Card 1: Car gallery

**Your story.** As somebody who likes cars, I want to look through cool cars and
their stats, so that I can see which ones are fastest and why.

**Done when:**
- [ ] Every car shows its name and a picture
- [ ] Clicking a car shows its year, top speed and horsepower
- [ ] You can get back to the list

**Build it:** Architecture, Feature 1. **Build this one first.** It is the
quickest win in the whole app.

**Your job before you code:** pick the cars. Five is plenty to start. They are
your app's personality, so choose ones you actually think are cool.

---

## Card 2: Parts library

**Your story.** As somebody who likes cars but does not know how they work, I
want to read what each part does, so that I understand what is happening under
the hood.

**Done when:**
- [ ] Every part has its own short page
- [ ] Each page says what the part does in plain words
- [ ] Each page says what happens if that part is upgraded

**Build it:** Architecture, Feature 2.

**Your job before you code:** write the part pages. Engine, turbo, exhaust,
brakes, tires is a good five. Three or four sentences each, in words you would
actually use. The two sites you picked out both do exactly this, so look at how
they explain things.

---

## Card 3: Horsepower builder

**Your story.** As somebody who wants a faster car, I want to pick upgrades and
watch the horsepower change, so that I can see what actually makes a car more
powerful.

**Done when:**
- [ ] You start with a stock engine and a starting horsepower number
- [ ] Every upgrade you pick adds horsepower
- [ ] The total updates as soon as you pick something
- [ ] You can take an upgrade back off and the number goes back down

**Build it:** Architecture, Feature 3.

This is the one you asked for. You told your teacher you like "what you can do
to give the car more horsepower", and this is that, on a screen.

---

## Card 4: Quiz

**Your story.** As a user, I want to take a quiz, so that I can test my knowledge
about the cars and parts I learned about.

*(This is your own story, the one you handed in, tidied up. It was written in
the first person using the author's own name as the user type, and a story names
a kind of person rather than one particular person, so it is now "As a user".
Everything else is yours.)*

**Done when:**
- [ ] The questions come from your parts pages
- [ ] You can pick an answer for each question
- [ ] You can press submit when you are done
- [ ] It tells you how many you got right

**Build it:** Architecture, Feature 4. Build it **last**, not because it is
unimportant but because it is the hardest, and the other three teach you
everything it needs.
