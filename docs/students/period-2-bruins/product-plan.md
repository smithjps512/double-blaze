# Product Plan

Team: Bruins

## Purpose

Building a game that helps with inspiring people to support homeless people.

## Who are the users

Anyone that enjoys this type of game.

## Product description

Players step into a dynamic action adventure where they fight bad guys and help
people in need. The game combines exciting combat with a powerful social
mission. Every level challenges players to protect communities and restore
hope.

The benefit of this game is to inspire people to help the homeless while playing
it. You will not be bored.

## Features

**Levels:** Every level challenges players to protect communities and restore
hope.

**Combat:** School appropriate weapons to fight bad guys.

**Hand out supplies:** You walk the streets of, let's say, downtown Blacksburg,
and hand out food and drinks to homeless people.

**Shop:** You go in a store and there are prices. It is 1 dollar for a bottle of
water, 2 for a canned drink and 3 for a fountain drink.

**Upgrades:** You buy things that make you able to buy more things for the
homeless.

**Ranks:** The rankings are Homeless Helper 1, then Homeless Helper 2, then
Homeless Helper 3, then Homeless Helper 4, then Homeless Helper Boss, and then
Homeless Helper God.

**Reset and replay:** When you get to the end of the game you get points to use.
You reset the game and use the game points to get things from the shop.
