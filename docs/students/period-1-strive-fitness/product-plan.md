# Strive Fitness

Team: BMS Crew

## Purpose

An app that encourages you to be active, while doing everyday activities. Tell
you the weather so you can know if there are good conditions for physical
activities. Telling you about nearby areas with the nutrition your body needs
after being active.

## Who are the users

**Consumer:** This app is for people who want to become more active, fit, take
care of the environment, and/or eat healthier.

## Features

**Signup/login:** The app will let you sign up for the app. The user will sign
up through Google, email, or Apple. Using Apple, Google, or email will make
signup less challenging because you have the choice between them.

**A chat room to see who has done the most physical activity:** The app will be
able to have a chat room where you can look at other people's activity and post
what you have been doing. It will allow users to be more social and encourage
them to do more activities.

**A credit system:** The app will have a credit system. You can earn credits by
doing activity and you can spend credits on cooler customization options. It
would make users want to do more things so they can customize their space.

**Food suggestions:** Our app will be able to give suggestions for healthy
nutritious food suggested by your peers. Will have moderation and a report
button for users to alert of bad options. It allows users to have healthier
foods without having to find any online, so after their activities they can have
nutritious food.

**Interactive ai:** It would search all the activities for what you are looking
to do. So you can see what is best for you to do around you.

**Ads:** To get money to continue creating the app. We can fund the app more.

**Subscription tiers:** Bronze, no ads, $5 a month. Gold, Bronze plus multi user
access, $15. We can fund the app.

**Ability to change the background colors:** You will have the ability to change
the color of the background to what suits you the best. It helps the app feel
more personalized.

**Weather app:** To see if outside is good for the activities. Because if there
is a category 5 tornado, if you check the weather app you won't die. So we can
save lives and get our app promoted.
