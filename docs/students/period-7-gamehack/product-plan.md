# GAMEHACK

Team: GameHack

## Purpose

An app that can help you get past a difficult part in a game, or make the game
easier and more fun. It helps kids get past difficult parts of the game.

## Who are the users

**Gamers:** People who play video games in general, and people who want to know
how to beat a level or make it easier.

**Kids:** Players who get stuck and want to finish the game faster.

**Modders:** People interested in learning more about how a game works.

## Product description

An app that has many features to help get past difficult parts in games. It can
give people video game tips and help them beat specific hard parts of video
games using an AI that calculates that part of the game.

## Features

**AI:** An AI that calculates methods and tutorials for specific hard parts, so
people can get easy access to the parts they might be stuck on.

**Tutorials:** Showing how to do something, in this case teaching how to beat a
game or a level, so users can accomplish a goal.

**Tips:** Video game tips for a specific part of a game.

**Maps:** Maps for finding your way around a game.

**Cheat codes:** Codes that make a game easier.

**Mods:** Modifications that change how a game plays.
