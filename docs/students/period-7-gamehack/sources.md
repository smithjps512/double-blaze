# Where this team's documents came from

Transcribed from Google Drive, folder `7th period / 7th period app / gamehack`.

- `app product plan.pdf`, `app product plan2.pdf`, `app product plan4.pdf`,
  `app product plan5.pdf`: four product plans for the same product, one per
  student. The plan here is the union of all four, using their sentences.
  `app product plan3.pdf` is a byte-identical re-upload of plan 2 and was
  skipped.
- `My User Stories.pdf`, `My User Stories2.pdf`, `My User Stories3.pdf`: three
  user stories. Tutorials twice, and the AI feature.

**Removed in transcription, and worth knowing about:** `app product plan.pdf`
ended with a teacher's grading comment about an individual student ("Teacher
Grade for today is an A..."), including a remark about their classroom
behaviour. That is a record about a child, it is not part of the product plan,
and this repository is public. It is not reproduced here or anywhere in the
generated prototype.

Also changed: spelling corrected, the fill-in-the-blank template scaffolding
removed. Nothing was added.

One thing for the team to settle: `My User Stories2.pdf` says "There are no
scenarios in our App, it just gives you links to video game tutorials", which
contradicts the AI feature the other two students wrote stories for. They should
decide whether GAMEHACK is a link directory or an AI product.
