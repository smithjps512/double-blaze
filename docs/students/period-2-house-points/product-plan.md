# House Point Tracker

Team: House Points

## Purpose

Track the points of all houses at BMS. It helps track the number of points each
house has in real time so students can check it.

## Who are the users

**Students:** They can view house points for the different houses, along with a
list of house member names. They use a guest account.

**Teachers:** They are the only ones who can add points, so they sign in with
their own account.

## Product description

When you open the app you see the current point tallies of each house. If you
click on a house, it shows further information about the house including the
students in it. The app has many features including instant point updates,
scheduled points by teachers, and the daily amount given and gained by each
teacher, student and house.

## Features

**View house points:** See the current point tallies of every house in real
time.

**House detail:** Click a house to see more about it, including the students in
it.

**Teacher sign in:** Teachers sign in with a unique username and a shared
password. Students use a guest account that needs no username or password.

**Add points:** Teachers award points to a student when the student does
something good.

**Point cap:** A maximum number of points a teacher can add at one time, so
somebody who gets hold of the password cannot add a pile of points.

**Cloud storage:** Points are stored in the cloud so they are not lost when a
teacher clears their cookies, and the totals stay correct and attached to the
right house.

**Change logs:** A record of who recently changed the point count.

**Point animations:** A small, short animation plays when an individual gains
points, which you can close, and which you can turn off in settings.
