# Where this team's documents came from

Transcribed from Google Drive, folder `2nd period / house points`.

- `Create Your Product Planhousepoints4.pdf` and
  `Create Your Product Planhousepointsplan.pdf`: two plans for the same product,
  one per student. The plan here is the union of both.
- `User Story Examplehousepoints.pdf`: teacher accounts and adding points.
- `User Story Examplehousepoints2.pdf`: point animations.
- `User Story Examplejousepoints3.pdf`: cloud storage.
- `User Story Example.pdf`: the point adding cap.

**Removed in transcription:** four students' first names, and in one case a full
name, which headed the documents. This repository is public.

Also changed: spelling corrected, template scaffolding removed. Nothing was
added.

Note for the team: one plan left "Who are your users" and "Features" completely
blank; the other filled them in. The union is used here.
