# Bus Buddy

Team: Sample Team

This is a worked example, written the way a real team would write it, including
the mistakes. One feature has no user story and one story has no "so that", so
the coach notes in the generated prototype have something true to say.

## Purpose

Students at our school wait outside for buses that are late, early, or already
gone, and nobody tells them which. Parents call the front office to ask where
the bus is, and the office cannot answer either. Bus Buddy tells everybody the
same thing at the same time so nobody is standing in the rain guessing.

## Who are the users

**Students:** They want to know how many minutes until their bus reaches their
stop, and whether it is worth leaving class early to catch it. They are on
phones, they are in a hurry, and they will not read anything long.

**Parents:** They want to know their kid got on the bus and when the bus will
reach their neighborhood. They mostly want to stop calling the school.

**Bus drivers:** They want to report a delay once and have it reach everyone,
instead of answering the same question forty times.

**Front office staff:** They want to see every route at once so they can answer
a phone call in ten seconds instead of ten minutes.

## Product description

Bus Buddy is a phone app that shows where school buses are and when they will
arrive. Students set their bus number and their stop, and the app shows a
countdown. Drivers can report a delay in two taps, and every rider on that route
sees it immediately. The front office gets a board with every route on it.

## Features

**Bus tracker:** Shows the countdown to your bus reaching your stop, and where
the bus is right now.

**Delay alerts:** Drivers report a delay, and every rider on that route is told
right away with the reason and the new time.

**My stop setup:** Lets a rider choose their bus number and their stop once, so
the app knows what to show them.

**Route board:** A screen for the front office showing every route and its
status at the same time.

**Ride history:** A record of which buses a student rode and when. We have not
written a story for this one yet.
