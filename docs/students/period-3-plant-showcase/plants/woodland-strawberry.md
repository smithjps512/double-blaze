## Plant page

A woodland strawberry (*Fragaria vesca*) is a flower in the rose family.
Naturally occurring across the Northern Hemisphere, it is also known as a wild
strawberry and wood strawberry. It has dark green leaves and bright red fruits.
The fruits are small yet flavorful.
