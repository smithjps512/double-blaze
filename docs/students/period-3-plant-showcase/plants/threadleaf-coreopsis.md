# Threadleaf Coreopsis

- Botanical name: *Coreopsis verticillata*
- Sources: photos of two handwritten pages (Drive
  `1_E4nO5_0cF61Gf75AngQTpnOcxqpVbaz` and `1SaaGlUWclji1XnbdHuaPcjjCi_ZNy7DG`)
- Pages supplied: plant page and care page
- Status: complete. The student drew the plant rather than supplying a photo.

## Plant page

1. It handles extreme summer heat, drought and even road salt with ease once
   established.
2. It won Perennial Plant of the Year in 1992 from the Perennial Plant
   Association.
3. It is native to the southern United States, in states such as Florida and
   Texas.

## Care page

1. Low maintenance.
2. Thrives best in full sun, 6 to 8 hours every day.
3. Poor, rocky or sandy soil.
4. 1 inch of water every week.
5. Little to no fertilizer needed.
6. Good drainage of soil.
7. Wet or soggy soil harms the plant. Water regularly until the roots take hold.

## Notes for the teacher

  reading "please find a picture on Google". The drawing is the better answer

  on the page. It has been written out above as the Perennial Plant Association
