## Plant page

### Appearance and size

- Height: 1 to 4 feet tall.
- Foliage: leaves are rough and hairy, growing up to six inches.
- Plant type depends on how it is treated, which decides how long it will live.

### Facts and general information

- It self seeds.
- It has high drought tolerance and can adapt.
- It thrives in sun.

## Care page

How to take care of a black-eyed Susan.

1. Plant them in full sun so they get at least six hours of sun.
2. Use loose, well draining soil. They dislike sitting in soggy soil.
3. Water plants regularly during the growing season.
