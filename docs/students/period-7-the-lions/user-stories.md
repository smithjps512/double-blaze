## Daily routine

As a user, I want to stay organized and gain good habits, so that I will be a
better person overall.

  - User should gain good habits
  - User should stay organized
  - User should know what they missed
  Given I am a user who is trying to build good habits
  When I use the app to make good habits
  Then it will work properly and actually help me
  Given I am trying to keep track of my schedule
  When I open the app to check my schedule
  Then I can see what I have done and what I have to do
  Given I am trying to lose weight
  When I use the app to get an eating routine
  Then I will get an eating routine optimized to help me lose weight

## Nutrition planner and scanner

As a person wanting to get more fit and change their diet, I want to know what
foods will be the most beneficial for my goals, so that I can grow and be the
best version of myself.

  - Person should be able to set goals and get food-related outputs
  - Scanner should be able to tell the person what they will get out of the food
  - Scanner should adjust for the person's goal
  Given someone wants to eat more protein to help build muscle
  When they scan a food label
  Then it will tell them if it is high in protein to help them with their goals
  Given someone wants to lose weight
  When they scan a food label
  Then it will tell them if the food is healthy or not

## AI helper

As a person looking to get stronger, I want to know whether lots of weight and
few reps or little weight and lots of reps is better, and other general tips, so
that I can maximize the amount of muscle I grow and be the best version of
myself.

  - Person needs to be able to ask a lot of questions
  - AI needs to constantly respond correctly
  - AI needs to be helpful based on different situations
  - AI should be programmed to think of general things that humans like
  Given someone wants strength tips
  When they ask the AI about weights that are helpful for their goals
  Then the AI will respond with good helpful answers and tips
  Given someone does not know where to start
  When they ask the AI about how to build up to a pushup
  Then the AI will tell them helpful advice like planks
