# Daily

Team: The Lions

## Purpose

An app that encourages you to be active while doing everyday activities. It
tells you the weather so you can know if there are good conditions for physical
activities, and tells you about nearby areas with the nutrition your body needs
after being active.

## Who are the users

**Unorganized people who need reminders:** People who need a daily routine.

**People trying to build good habits:** People who want to create good habits
and keep them.

## Product description

A simplistic app for people who need a daily routine and people who want to
create good habits. It is very compatible with all types of disabilities, and it
has many features including dieting control, it can make a workout routine, and
you have an AI helper.

## Features

**Daily routine:** A simple routine to follow, so people stay organized.

**AI helper:** Chat with an AI about productivity, diets and general tips.

**Nutrition planner:** Dieting control and nutritional planning.

**Workout routine:** It can make a workout routine for you.

**Weather:** Tells you the weather so you know if conditions are good for being
active outside.

**Share your routine:** Share your routine with other people.

**Rewards:** Rewards for keeping up with your habits.
