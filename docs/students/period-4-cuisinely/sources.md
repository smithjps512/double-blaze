## The delivery story was rewritten, 9 September

A second `My User Stories.pdf`, handed in after the first pass. It replaces the
Delivery story entirely and the other two are untouched.

**The narrative and the three acceptance criteria are unchanged.** What is new
is six numbered scenarios, and they are a considerable step up from anything
this team had written before: signing up, ordering, nutrition, reading reviews,
a restaurant owner adding their business, and how the star rating works.

Two of those had been the biggest holes in the whole set. Reviews had no story
at all, and the restaurant owner, who is half the reason the app exists
according to their own plan, had never been written about. Both are now on the
page in their own words.

Transcribed as written, including "Example Restaurant", which is their
placeholder rather than a real place. Changed: "ect." to "etc.", "e-mail" to
"email", "shouldn't" to "should not", and the six scenarios kept their numbering
because the source numbers them.

**Not changed, deliberately:** the scenarios sit inside a story whose narrative
is about ordering a delivery, and two of them are not about ordering a delivery.
Splitting them is theirs to do and their build cards now say so, with the split
spelled out. Doing it for them would take away the finding.

`Revised:` is set on the stories and `Card updated:` on the cards, which is what
keeps the two in step and makes the What next page say so when they are not.
