# Cuisinely

Team: Cuisinely

## Purpose

To show all restaurants in the area, and the menu.

## Who are the users

**People looking for a place to eat locally:** They are trying to keep track of
calories and other information.

**Smaller restaurant owners:** They need a way to advertise their company
without getting overrun by bigger businesses.

## Product description

The app will allow you to view the schedules and menus of each restaurant, as
well as the ratings and amount of calories in each food item. Users will be able
to see all the reviews, ratings, and schedules of the local, smaller
restaurants. They will also have an accessible menu with all the calories and
other important information. This app will also have the ability to deliver.

## Features

**Maps with locations of restaurants:** Needed. See where the local restaurants
are, with their ratings.

**Delivery:** Needed. Order from a restaurant and have it delivered to your
address.

**Calories and information of food:** Needed. Nutrition facts and other
information for each item on a menu.

**Rating and reviews:** Optional. Reviews and ratings for the local, smaller
restaurants.
