# The Dirt Bikes user stories

Team: The Dirt Bikes

Revised: 2026-09-17

## Order a track

As a user, I want to buy a dirt bike track, so that I can ride whenever I want.

  - They must have enough space on their land
  - They must have a dirt bike or any bike
  - They must have enough money because it is expensive
  Given I make an account then customize and order the track
  When I click order
  Then it says it has been ordered, they prepare the track, deliver it to my property and build it

## Jump angle

As a person with a dirt bike, I want to choose the angle of a jump, so that I can use that jump with my dirt bike.

  - I must have added jumps to my customization
  - I must have the right app
  Given I have a track that has a jump
  When I choose the angle
  Then the builder builds that jump with that angle

## Jump gap

As a person with a dirt bike, I want to choose the gap between two jumps, so that I can clear the jumps.

  - I must have added more than 1 jump to my customization
  - I must have the right app
  - I must have a bike that can hit jumps
  Given I add 2 or more jumps
  When I choose the gap between the jumps
  Then the builder makes the gap between the jumps the right distance

## Bike Compatibility Check

As a user, I want to make sure my bike works on the track and jumps that i am buying, so that I dont buy a track that I cant ride on because my bike is too big or small.

  - I must know what bike I have
  - I have to have enough money to buy the track that works

  Given I customized my track
  When I put my bike in the checker
  Then it shows if my bike is compatible with the track

## Send in Pictures

As a user, I want to send in pictures of my land, so that se the builders can get a better idea of how much materials they need and how big your land is.

  - The page has to have a pictures box
  - If I click on the box I can choose from my photo library or my camera
  - The pictures must be send to the builders in a check able quality

  Given I want to send pictures to the builders for more accurate building
  When I click on the pictures box I can chose a photo from my library or take a picture
  Then the builders see it and can get a better idea of what they need

## Bike Checker

As a Person with a dirt bike, I want to make sure that my bike can ride my track, so that if I ride the track I don't crash my bike.

  - I must have a bike
  - The bike I have must be on the list
  - The bike must be able to ride jumps

  Given I have a bike
  When I put my bike into the checker on the app
  Then the app tells me if the bike will work on the track I made

## Picture Adding

As a Person with a dirt bike and land, I want to make sure that the builder gets the amount of land I have accurate, so that the builder bring enough materials and don't over or under estimate my land.

  - I must have camera
  - I must send in a picture that is nice enough so the builders can check it

  Given I have a camera
  When I send the pictures to the builder
  Then the builder has a better idea of how much land I have to build on
