# Your Template

Team: The Dirt Bikes

## Purpose

So people can have their own dirt bike track tailored to their needs, and it
would be easier to ride. People need cool dirt bike tracks.

## Who are the users

**People who have dirt bikes:** Riders who want a track at their own place.

**People who build the tracks:** The builders who bring in dirt and construct
the track on the land.

## Product description

You download an app on your phone and customize a dirt bike track that fits the
amount of space you have. You send in pictures of the place you want to build it
so the builder can see how much space there is. People then bring in dirt and
build the track on the land.

## Features

**Draw the track:** The track is fully customizable and you have the option to
draw it.

**Jump angle:** You can choose the angle of the jumps.

**Jump gap:** You can choose the gap between the jumps.

**Bike check:** The app can tell you if your bike can make the jump.

**Send in pictures:** Send photos of where you want the track and how big you
want it, so the builder can see the space.
