# Where this team's documents came from

Transcribed from Google Drive, folder `2nd period / dirt bikes`.

- `Create Your Product Plandirtbike.pdf`, `Create Your Product Plandirtbike2.pdf`
  and `Create Your Product Plandirtbike 3.pdf`: three plans for the same
  product, one per student. The plan here is the union of all three.
- `User Story Exampledirtbike.pdf`: ordering a track.

**Removed in transcription:** three students' first names heading the user
story, and a teacher's grade and comment left on one plan ("B- to much
talking"). A grade on a named student is a record about a child rather than part
of the product plan, and this repository is public. A stray "HI" typed into
another plan was also dropped.

Also changed: spelling corrected, template scaffolding removed. Nothing was
added, so the plan's missing app name and the single user story stay as they are
and the prototype reports both.
