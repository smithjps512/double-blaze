# Your Template

Team: Team Orangutan

## Purpose

To teach people math so they can get smarter.

## Who are the users

People who need help with math, which is basically everyone, because you can
always improve.

## Product description

An app that teaches math. Different courses on different subjects of math, for
example addition and subtraction, trigonometry, 2 digit multiplication,
factoring any kind of polynomial of higher degrees, and graph theory, with
videos and guides on how to do it.

## Features

**Video:** A video in the course to help visual learners understand math, so it
teaches people math and they get smarter.

**Quiz:** A quiz at the end of the course, so it helps you get more familiar
with the mathematical subjects and get more knowledge.

**Placement test:** It sorts users based on skill level, so the experience is
not too complicated or too easy.

**AI assistant:** You put in a mathematical equation and it gives the answer,
like a calculator for everything. It also gives you the steps to solve it, so
you learn more about problems you do not know.

**Secret age groups:** After the placement test, users get assigned to an age
group. They do not get told. This way everyone gets a personalized experience
with the appropriate level of challenge.

**Leaderboard:** A leaderboard for the most XP earned, because competition
encourages players to keep playing.

**Racing game against AI:** A racing game against an AI slightly more advanced
than your level, so students work harder to beat it.

**Dictionary:** A dictionary for many mathematical subjects and theorems, a
handy reference for students confused about a word.

**Secret mode:** Accessed by pressing a key combination. A prank where
calculations are off by one, so 1 plus 1 is 3. No benefits, just funny.
