# Product Plan

Team: CTOS

Proposal, 25 September. Not adopted yet: the team's own plan is
`product-plan.md` one folder up.

## Purpose

Students who have just moved to a school in a new country, and are still
learning its language, often cannot talk to the people around them. They
understand more than they can say, they miss what is going on, and making a
friend has to wait until their English is good enough.

This app lets everyone at a school write in their own language and read in
theirs, in real time, so a new student can talk to classmates from their first
day, and learn the language by using it.

## Who are the users

**New students:** students in their first months at the school who are still
learning English. They use the app in their own language: Mandarin, Spanish,
or any other it supports.

**Classmates:** students who already speak English, and who want to talk to a
new student without a shared language getting in the way. Many of them will
use it to practise a language too.

**Buddies:** students who volunteer to help a new student in their first weeks.
Often they speak the same first language and arrived a year or two earlier.

Everyone is a student at the same school. Nobody from outside the school can
message a student.

## Product description

A chat app for one school. Every message is translated for whoever reads it,
so a group chat can have a Mandarin speaker, a Spanish speaker and an English
speaker all talking at once, each reading in their own language. The original
is always one tap away, because translation is sometimes wrong.

Around the chat are the things a new student needs in their first weeks:
quick phrases for the hallway, short guides to how the school works, a school
map in their language, practice on the phrases they use most, and a buddy.

Stickers, reactions and avatars are there because they say things without
needing words. Streaks are there because talking every day is how a language
is learned.

## Features

**Choose your language:** The first screen asks which language you speak, in
that language. The whole app, not just the messages, switches to it.

**Translated messaging:** Direct messages between two people. Everyone writes
in their own language and reads in theirs. Every translated message has Show
original.

**Group chats in any language:** Group chats of 2 to 30 people, where every
member reads every message in their own language.

**Calls with live captions:** A voice call where what the other person says
appears as captions in your language.

**Quick phrases:** A set of ready-made phrases for school, like "Can you say
that more slowly?" or "Where is room 204?", in every language. Tap one and it
goes into your message.

**Stickers and reactions:** Say something without words. The sticker tray and
reactions work the same in every language.

**Streaks:** Days in a row you have talked to somebody. A streak with your
buddy is practice every day.

**School guides:** Short guides to how the school works: lunch, the bus, hall
passes, what a word like "tardy" means. Every guide is in every language, and
can be read aloud.

**Practice:** Quizzes on the phrases you need, in the language you are
learning. Scored with the team's grade bands.

**School map:** A map of the school with every room named in your language and
in English, and a button to ask somebody the way.

**Buddies and clubs:** Get matched with a buddy, and join a club for your
language or for the language you are learning.

**Avatar:** Build a model of yourself, so people can see who you are.

**Turn features off and on:** Turn off the features you do not use.

**Safety and support:** Report or block anyone from inside a chat, and find
help in your own language.
