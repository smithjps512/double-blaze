# CTOS user stories

Team: CTOS

Revised: 2026-09-10

## In app messaging and calls

Ever wanted an app that also focuses on messaging and calls? Call anyone, text
anyone. You do not have to go to a separate application or tab with this. You
and other people are assigned a number combo in this arrangement only:
(1-15)-###-###-###_### . You will find your combo in the dedicated section of
the app, In-App Messaging and Calls.

As a user, I always wanted an app that has an in app call and messaging
feature, so that I can text and call my friends, whenever I desire.

## Avatar

Avatars are a 3D model of what you customize to your style, pretty much like
Snapchat. You will find this in your profile in settings.

As a user, I always wanted a good way to show others a cool model of themselves
or others, so that others can display themselves freely, instead of just a
plain ol' profile picture.

## Shorts and videos

Shorts are short form content: videos 75 seconds or less. It brings more
engagement and entertainment than long form, anything longer than 75 seconds.
Shorts are technically videos, but in this app, videos are long form. Shorts and
videos are always found in Media.

As a user, I want to add a video, so that I can share it with the world.

  - I can upload the video
  - It accepts many video formats
  - I can share my video
  Given I have recorded a video I want to share
  When I select create
  Then I see a box that says "upload video here" and I can upload the video

  Given I have uploaded my video
  When I see the box that says title
  Then I can name my video

As a user, I always wanted a platform where you can post videos for select
people to watch or all, so that others can enjoy the entertainment this app
gives.

## Topics

Topics are topics that are read, and accompanied with or without a short,
video, and/or quiz (gamified, traditional, or both). Topics are informational
subjects ranging from lots: history, math, science, social media, English, law,
etc.

As a user, I always wanted to read, play or take quizzes, or watch, all
simultaneously, so I decided to create this just to fit it, and hopefully others
can enjoy this.

## Map

The map feature shows you the nearest places tailored to what you want, for
example, nearest fast food, park, etc. It also shows you where your friends are
(if they have their location on). Uses Google Maps.

As a user, I constantly wanted a map to know where to go, where others are, and
more.

## Streaks

Streaks are unbroken tasks, specifically calling and chatting. For example, a
50 day streak is an unbroken streak for chatting and or calling.

As a user, I continually always wanted to chat, but never had a motivation to,
so that I can chat and call, and bond with select people.

## User support

This is pretty much just customer support. This feature allows you to report
bugs to developers and fix it.

As a user, I want to know how to fix things, get to things, etc., so that I
know what to do instead of blind guessing what to do.

## Turn features off and on

Ever wanted to turn off features, especially the ones that you do not want, or
turn on features that you do want, or accidentally turning off a feature? Well,
we do have that here.

As a user, I desire a feature that allows me to turn off and or on features,
especially the ones that are plain useless, or useful, so that would give me
more user autonomy and improve successfulness.

## Stickers and reaction images

Ever wanted to show others images: memes, evidence, photos, etc? Well, here are
those same exact features. You can create stickers or images, or take it from
someone.

As a user, I desired a way to express myself online with pictures too, so that I
can express myself way better than just plain old text.

## Public and private communities

Ever wanted to make a public or private group chat, where you can: send videos,
screenshare, chat, call, etc.

As a user, I always wanted a community in which I can make a public community
for anyone to join, or a private community for me and my friends, so that I can
bond with others that have the same preferences as me, and or just to chat with
friends.

## Quizzing

As a user, I want a fun or traditional quiz to show my knowledge on things, so
that I can be knowledgeable about it.

  - A 96 to 100 is Awesome, impressive, does not need to study
  - A 91 to 95 is Impressive, great, could be applied
  - B 71 to 90 is Great, study, needs improvement
  - C 51 to 70 is Decent, study, needs improvement
  - D 31 to 50 is Moderate, study, needs improvement
  - E 11 to 30 is Substandard, study, needs improvement
  - F 0 to 10 is Terrible, study, needs improvement
  - Click or tap Create to start building your quiz, then choose Traditional or Gamified
  - Traditional is like a test, Gamified is like Blooket, Gimkit or Kahoot
  - The plus button makes questions, the minus button deletes a selected question, some selected up to 5, or all
  - The question layout has the Question and the Answers
  - Click or tap the arrow button to finalize the quiz
  - A quiz can be public, anyone can find and use it, or private, certain people can find and use it by link
  - Creating a quiz would be found on Encyclopedia in the main app
