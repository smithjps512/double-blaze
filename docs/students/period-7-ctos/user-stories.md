## Shorts and videos

As a user, I want to add a video, so that I can share it with the world.

  - I can upload the video
  - It accepts many video formats
  - I can share my video
  Given I have recorded a video I want to share
  When I select create
  Then I see a box that says "upload video here" and I can upload the video

  Given I have uploaded my video
  When I see the box that says title
  Then I can name my video

## Quizzing

As a user, I want a fun or traditional quiz to show my knowledge on things, so
that I can be knowledgeable about it.

  - A 96 to 100 is Awesome, impressive, does not need to study
  - A 91 to 95 is Impressive, great, could be applied
  - B 71 to 90 is Great, study, needs improvement
  - C 51 to 70 is Decent, study, needs improvement
  - D 31 to 50 is Moderate, study, needs improvement
  - E 11 to 30 is Substandard, study, needs improvement
  - F 0 to 10 is Terrible, study, needs improvement
  - Click or tap Create to start building your quiz, then choose Traditional or Gamified
  - Traditional is like a test, Gamified is like Blooket, Gimkit or Kahoot
  - The plus button makes questions, the minus button deletes a selected question, some selected up to 5, or all
  - The question layout has the Question and the Answers
  - Click or tap the arrow button to finalize the quiz
  - A quiz can be public, anyone can find and use it, or private, certain people can find and use it by link
  - Creating a quiz would be found on Encyclopedia in the main app
