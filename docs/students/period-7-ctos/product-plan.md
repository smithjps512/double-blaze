# Product Plan

Team: CTOS

## Purpose

An app that combines Wikipedia, YouTube, TikTok and other elements. The app is
an informational and social media app.

## Who are the users

Everyone and anyone, specifically anyone who wants a combination of the apps
listed above and their features too.

## Product description

This app combines the elements of Wikipedia, YouTube, TikTok, Snap and Discord.
You read informative topics, and you can also watch videos, text and call.
Wikipedia becomes Encyclopedia: topics you can read, with sources, photos and
videos to explain them, possibly with a quiz feature. YouTube becomes Media:
long form videos and content for engagement. TikTok becomes Media Shorts: short
form videos and content. Snap is merged with Discord into Chats: group, public
or private, message chat and call.

## Features

**In app messaging and calls:** Direct and private messages between 2 and 30
people, group chat, and calls and texts in the app.

**Avatar:** Design your own body, so people can express themselves.

**Shorts and videos:** Short form content and long form content, for
entertainment.

**Topics:** Reading informative topics like how to guides.

**Map:** Know where you should go and where others are.

**What you should get:** What you should get if you want the best item that fits
it, for example a car that is low mileage. Best deals.

**Streaks:** A streak is how much you have been messaging or calling someone,
for example 5 days.

**LIVEs:** Livestreams, for fun and entertainment.

**Data protection and encryption:** Protect your security and data, delete
messages, and so on.

**User support:** FAQs, what to do if you encounter bugs and issues, and reviews
about what could be done better.

**Bots:** To add more livelihood into your messages and communities, and for
user support.

**Turn features off and on:** Turn off and on the features you do not want, for
user autonomy.

**Advanced reports and moderation:** For safety.

**Algorithm sorting:** Sorting algorithm videos, best for engagement.

**Stickers and reaction images:** Express yourselves better.

**Public and private communities:** Build a public community messaging group
chat, Discord based, or a private one for you and friends.

**Quizzing:** Quiz others or yourself, for engagement and knowledge.
