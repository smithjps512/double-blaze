# PC BROS

Team: The Dream Team

Draft: true

## Purpose

Gaming PCs are built for people with money. A student who saves up for months
still ends up choosing between a machine that is too expensive and one that will
not run the games they actually play, and nobody explains which of the two they
are buying.

PC BROS is for **kids like us**: pick your own parts, watch the price add up as
you go, and get a machine that is exactly the one you designed, hand built, with
your name on it if you want it there.

## Who are the users

**Students who game:** They know what games they want to play and have a
number in their head they cannot go over. They do not know what a GPU is called
or which one matters, and every site that could tell them is written for
somebody twice their age. They want to choose the parts themselves rather than
be sold a box.

**Whoever actually pays:** A parent, a grandparent, somebody at a birthday. They
are not buying the PC, they are approving it. They want to see what it costs
before they say yes, know what is in it, and be sure the thing that arrives is
the thing that was agreed.

**The PC BROS workshop:** Us. Somebody has to read the order, build that exact
machine, and say when it has shipped. We are a real part of how this works and
the app does not do anything for us yet, which is a hole worth knowing about
rather than one to be embarrassed by.

## Product description

PC BROS is a custom PC shop for students and young people. You choose your own
parts on our site, the price updates as you pick each one, and when you are
happy you place the order and we build it by hand.

Every machine is built to that exact order, with an engraving on the metal if
you want one, so what turns up is yours rather than whatever was in the
warehouse. You can watch your order's progress from ordered to built to shipped.
And if you would rather do it in person, our store is a real place you can walk
into and build it on the bench with us.

## Features

**Build your own PC:** Choose a CPU, graphics card, memory, storage and case
from what we stock, one at a time.

**The price as you go:** The running total updates every time you pick a part,
so you always know where you are against your budget before you commit to
anything.

**Order your build:** Check the whole build on one screen, add an engraving and
a delivery address, and place the order. An order missing a part or an address
is refused rather than half saved.

**Track your order:** See every order you have placed, newest first, and what
stage it has reached.

**Find our store:** Where the shop is, when it is open, and what happens if you
come in and build it on the bench instead of online.
