# PC BROS

Team: The Dream Team

## Purpose

So that kids like me have fun while gaming and can make a cool PC that they
like, for cheap money. This app lets you make your own PC that you like building
and more.

## Who are the users

**Kids who game:** Kids who want a PC they like, built cheaply.

## Features

**Build your own PC:** Building your own PC on our app, choosing the design and
how it looks.

**Come to our store:** Or you could come to our store and build it there.
