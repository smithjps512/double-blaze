## Card 1: Build my own PC

**Your story.** As a kid who games, I want to choose the parts for my own PC, so
that I get a machine I like at a price I can afford.

*(Your story said "As a PC company I want to make money so that I can be rich."
Every one of your acceptance criteria underneath it is something the company
does. A user story is written from the point of view of the person using the
app. Your customer is a kid who wants a good PC cheaply, and that is who this
rewrite is for. Notice that the app you would build for the customer is a
completely different app from the one you would build for the company.)*

**Done when:**
- [ ] You can choose a CPU, GPU, RAM, storage and case
- [ ] Each choice shows its price
- [ ] The running total updates as you choose

**Build it:** Architecture, Features 1 and 2. Build this first.

---

## Card 2: Order the PC I built

**Your story.** As a customer, I want to order the PC I designed, so that it
gets built and delivered to me.

**Done when:**
- [ ] The review screen lists every part you chose and the total
- [ ] Ordering with a part missing is refused
- [ ] Ordering with no address is refused
- [ ] You are told the order was placed

**Build it:** Architecture, Feature 3.

---

## Card 3: Check my order

**Your story.** As a customer, I want to see the orders I have placed, so that I
know what is coming and when.

**Done when:**
- [ ] Your orders appear, newest first
- [ ] Each shows what was ordered and its status

**Build it:** Architecture, Feature 4.

---

## A note for the whole team

**Your app is the smallest in the period and that is good.** Four features you
can actually finish beats seventeen you cannot start. Get all four working, then
add the engraving box, then add whatever you want. Finishing is a skill and not
many teams get to practise it.
