7th Period: CO2 Dragster Reflection

Intro to Technology. Students write an online reflection on the whole CO2 car project and turn it in to me. No passwords are needed today.

Write this on the board
doubleblaze.solutions/trail-crew/dragster/race-day/reflection

The Leaderboard tab at the top of that page shows race times, for question 1.2a. Please do not open the Teacher tab.

The 50 minutes

0 to 5Attendance. Students open the link on their own device.
5 to 8Read the box below aloud.
8 to 47Students write, quietly and on their own. Walk around. Point anyone stuck to the gray "Still to do" list at the bottom of the page.
47 to 50Anyone done presses Turn in my reflection and shows you the green Turned in screen.
Say to the class:
  1. Type your first and last name and pick Period 7.
  2. Answer every question. Each box shows a word count and turns green when you have written enough. Be specific: name real moments, tools and times.
  3. The Turn in button unlocks only when every answer is long enough. Repeating words to fill space will not count.
  4. Your answers save on this computer as you type. If you do not finish, use the same computer next class and pick up where you left off.

If something goes wrong

The page asks for a passwordThe site has not been unlocked. Students write their answers in a Google Doc and paste them in next class.
Turn in stays grayScroll to the "Still to do" list at the bottom. It names each answer that is too short.
Answers disappearedThey save only on the computer and browser where they were typed. Reopen the same link there. Never press "Start over".
Doesn't know their race timeCheck the Leaderboard tab, or write "don't know". That is fine.
Finishes earlyReread every answer and add a specific example or a "because" to each one. The word counts are the minimum.
Error when turning inWait a minute and press it again. The answers are still on the page.
