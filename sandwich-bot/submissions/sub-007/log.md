# sub-007: interpretation log

Goal: bowl of ramen. Result: A bowl of cooked noodles with a closed flavor packet in it. Chef Byte already ate some straight from the pot. The chili oil never came out.

What this student got right (say this first): water measured, boiled before
the noodles, a 3 minute timer, and the heat turned off. The best ramen
instructions in the class so far.

| Step | Words | Actions | Why |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | First get a pot that fits at least four cups of water | pickUp pot | Done as written. |
| 2 | Take a measuring cup and measure 2 cups of water | pickUp measuring-cup, fill measuring-cup | The student remembered the water, and measured it. |
| 3 | and dump them into the pot we got out not with the measuring cup | carry measuring-cup | Teacher's choice: 'not with the measuring cup' taken literally. Chef Byte carries the water in its hands. Splashes, but some lands in the pot. |
| 4 | put the pot on the stove | putDown pot | Done as written. |
| 5 | and put on a 9 | turnOn stove | Teacher's choice: read from context as the stove dial (not wearing a number 9). |
| 6 | and wait for the water to boil | wait | There is water this time, so it boils. |
| 7 | Once the water is boiling take the noodles out of the bag or box | check, takeOut noodles | Teacher's choice: naming the bag to take them out of counts as opening it. |
| 8 | then put the noodles in the water that is boiling | putIn noodles | Into boiling water: the noodles cook. |
| 9 | and set a timer for 3 minutes | setTimer timer | Done as written. |
| 10 | and stir occasionally | stir pot | No spoon named. Teacher's choice: safety stop, no hands in boiling water. |
| 11 | Once the 3 minute timer is up take the pot with the noodles and water of the heated surface | wait, putDown pot | 'of' read as 'off'. |
| 12 | and turn off the heat | turnOff stove | Done as written. |
| 13 | And add the flavor packet | putIn seasoning | Never opened, so the packet goes in whole (same rule as sub-003). |
| 14 | wait for the soup to cool to or add ice cubes and eat it like that | wait, eat pot | 'to' read as 'too'. Chef Byte picks the first choice (wait). Then it eats from the pot, because that is the order written. |
| 15 | add chili oil | pour chili-oil | The lid is still on. Nothing comes out. |
| 16 | pour it into a bowl and enjoy. | pour pot, eat bowl | The bowl comes from the cupboard. Chef Byte eats again. |
