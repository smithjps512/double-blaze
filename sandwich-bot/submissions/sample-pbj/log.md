# sample-pbj: interpretation log

| Step | Words | What Chef Byte does | Why |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Put peanut butter on the bread. | Puts the closed peanut butter jar on the closed bag of bread. | Nothing said to open the bag or the jar. The bread is still in the bag, so "the bread" means the bag. |
| 2 | Put jelly on the other bread. | Puts the closed jelly jar on top of the peanut butter jar. | There is no other bread out, so the other bread is still the bag, and the top of the bag already has a jar on it. |
| 3 | Put the bread together. | Shrugs: there is only one bread (the bag). | Impossible as written. |
| 4 | Cut it in half. | Robot chop. The bag of bread gets squished. | No knife named and none in hand. Silliest reasonable option, nobody gets hurt. |
| 5 | Enjoy! | Ta-da pose. | Robots don't eat. |

Fix cards: open bag, take out two slices, open each jar, pick up the knife,
flip the jelly slice, put a slice on the plate. With the first five inserted
before step 1 and "flip" before step 3, Chef Byte makes a real PB&J.
