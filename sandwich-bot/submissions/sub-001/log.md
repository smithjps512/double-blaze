# sub-001: interpretation log

| Step | Words | What Chef Byte does | Why |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | First take two piece of bread | Takes the whole closed bag. | "two piece" read kindly as "two pieces". The bag was never opened, so the pieces are in the bag. |
| 2 | then get peanut butter and jelly | Picks up both jars. Hands full, so the bag goes on the chef hat. | Three things, two hands. |
| 3 | and open the peanut butter jar | Sets the jelly jar on the counter, twists the peanut butter lid off. | Needs a free hand; the hat is taken. |
| 4 | and take your knife | Takes the butter knife. | "Your knife": the robot has none, so it takes the one on the counter. |
| 5 | and take some peanut butter with the sharp side | Scoops with the edge, gets a thin line. | Literal: the sharp side holds almost nothing. |
| 6 | and put some on the face of the bread | Draws a skinny peanut butter smiley face on the bag. | "The bread" is still the bag. "Face" read literally (teacher's choice). |
| 7 | and open the jelly | Puts the peanut butter jar down, opens the jelly. | Hands full again. |
| 8 | and put some on the other piece of bread on the face | Scoops jelly with its hand and draws a jelly face on the other half of the bag. | No tool said, so a hand. The other piece is still in the bag. |
| 9 | then put the jelly side with the peanut butter side. | Folds the bag in half so the faces touch. | Both "sides" are on the same bag. |

What this student got right (say this first in class): the jar gets opened before
scooping, a knife is named, and the two spread sides go together. Those are the
hard parts.
